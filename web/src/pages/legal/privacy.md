# Privacy policy

This page says what personal data Baseball Theater (BT) keeps, where it keeps it, and how you can download or delete it.

## Without an account

You can use BT without signing in. BT then keeps no account for you. Your browser keeps a few things for BT, such as whether you dismissed a notice; none of them identify you.

## With an account

When you sign in, BT keeps the following, each in the place named:

- BT database: users. Your email address, your display name, your role and what your account includes.
- BT database: settings. Your favorite teams and your preferences, such as Hide Scores and where videos play, so they follow you to every device.
- Logto. BT's sign-in service keeps your account and email address so it can send you sign-in codes.
- Cloud storage: data downloads. When you ask for a download of your data, BT keeps the file for 14 days, and the times you asked, so it can limit downloads to two in any 24 hours.
- Cloudflare Email. When your download is ready, BT emails you a link to it. The email holds the address it went to and no other personal data.
- Browser. Your browser keeps your sign-in session, a copy of your settings and the app's offline files, and, while you are signing in, the sign-in service's keys and the page to return to afterwards.

## What BT doesn't do

BT shows no advertising and uses no third-party analytics. Its server logs don't record your email address or other personal details.

## Download and delete

In Settings, under Your data, you can download everything BT keeps about you, and you can delete your account. Deleting it removes your data from every place listed above at once, signs you out, and clears what your browser kept for BT. Downloading and deleting are separate: you can do either without the other.

After a deletion, BT keeps only a record that an account with its internal ID was deleted, and when, so that a restored database backup can't bring your data back. Database backups age out on their own schedule, and the email and sign-in services keep their own logs for as long as their own policies say.

## Video counts

BT counts, for each video, how many times a day it is started and how many times it is watched to the end. These counts are anonymous: they record nothing about who watched.
