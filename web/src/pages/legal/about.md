# About

Baseball Theater is a place to follow Major League Baseball games: every game of the day on one scoreboard, each game's plays, pitches and box score as it happens, and the game's highlight videos.

## Who it's for

Fans who want to keep up with a game, catch up on one they missed, or watch its best moments, without spoilers when they don't want them: Hide Scores keeps every score and record out of sight.

## Your account

You can use Baseball Theater without an account. Signing in with your email lets you keep your favorite teams and settings on every device you use. You can download or delete everything Baseball Theater keeps about you from Settings, under Your data.

## Where the data comes from

Game data and videos come from Major League Baseball's public data feeds. Baseball Theater is an independent fan site and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Major League Baseball or any of its teams.

## Get in touch

Questions, ideas and problem reports are welcome on r/BaseballTheater, linked as Support & Community at the foot of every page.
