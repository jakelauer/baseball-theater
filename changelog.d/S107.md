- **Before, choosing Win Probability for a game's corner of the scoreboard had nothing to show: the approved design drew the odds only before first pitch, when no source gives them. Now there is an approved design for the odds while a game is live and after it ends — a small line of how the game swung, with each team's current chance while it's live — what it shows with scores hidden, and a game that hasn't started simply has no box. The app doesn't show it yet; S108 builds it.** Details: **S107** — shell-scoreboard amendment A7, ratified by the owner 2026-11-19; its slot occupant is one Figma component set, `slot-win-probability`.
