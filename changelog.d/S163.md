title: Backlog reviews now read what changed instead of the whole backlog
---

- **Before, every review of the plan (the backlog review, the check that the product docs still match it, and the review of newly approved designs) read every story and every open GitHub issue, so its cost grew with the backlog and would become too slow and costly once it held hundreds of items. Now each review starts from a short list of what changed since the last one and the stories and issues tied to it, reads each issue once after it opens or changes, and records the searches it ran, so its cost follows how much changed.** Nothing in the app changes. Details: **S163** — `pnpm backlog:search` (`scripts/backlog-vault/search.ts`: queries over a `BacklogSource`, `seed backlog|parity|intake`, `issues --since` through REST); `--json` events from `scripts/review-debt.sh` and `scripts/parity-debt.sh`; `scripts/audit.sh review|parity` require `query:` lines; the three review skills, their agents, `CLAUDE.md` and the backlog rules rescoped.
