title: How far live updates sit from the database, measured
---

- **Before, nobody knew how long the live game updaters and the minute-by-minute background jobs spend waiting on the database, or whether running far from it slows live scores. Now each of them reports how many trips to the database it made and how long they took, and the staging site places its live game updaters next to the database, so the numbers show whether the real site should do the same.** Details: **S220** — one `db-round-trips` log line per ingest object, hub, day hub and cron-job database scope (`inDatabaseScope`'s `ScopeLabel`, read by `kind` in `wrangler tail` or Workers Logs); ingest objects got only through `gameIngestStub` with staging's `BT_INGEST_LOCATION_HINT` (`enam`) and staging's `placement.hint` (`aws:us-east-2`); production unchanged apart from the log lines (from issue #538).
