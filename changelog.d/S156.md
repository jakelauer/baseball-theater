title: Several backlog items can now be built at once without tripping over each other

- **Before, the agents building Baseball Theater worked on one backlog item at a time, each stacked on the last, because every item rewrote the same few shared records and one change to the review tools held up everything above it; the 2026-11-19 run's eleven pull requests took about 28.5 hours to land. Now each item gets its own copy of the code with its own ports, keeps its own record files that merge in any order, and an overseer schedules two items at a time (three after two clean runs), settles problems shared between them once, gathers your questions into one list ordered by how much they block, and runs reviews alongside the work instead of pausing it.** Nothing in the app changes. Details: **S156** — `scripts/worktree-for.sh` and per-worktree ports (fixes #110); graders out of the reviewer hold (law L12); ledger entries as `docs/v3/audit/` fragments with `AUDIT.md` frozen, changelog entries as `changelog.d/` fragments, the As of line generated from each note's **Baseline:** line, and records proven merge-safe in any order (law L13); the generated `BACKLOG.md` retired and `INDEX.md`/`audit.jsonl` built rather than committed (law L9 reworded); `story_own_diff`; `claims.ts` (claims, barriers, shared findings, question order); `/run-backlog` rewritten around them.
