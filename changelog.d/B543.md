title: A corrected past day or game now refreshes at the edge on staging and production
---

- **When a past day's scores or a finished game are corrected, fans on staging and production now see the correction right away instead of an old copy for up to 30 days.** The edge purge added in S212 had never run there, because its two Cloudflare secrets never reached those servers. Details: **B543** (#543) — `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` maps the `BT_CF_PURGE_TOKEN` and `BT_CF_ZONE_ID` GitHub secrets into the `staging` and `production` jobs, and `workerSecretNames` (`functions/src/deploy/bootstrap.ts`, new `EDGE_PURGE_WORKER_SECRETS`) pushes both as Worker secrets for those two envs only (previews are on workers.dev, off the zone); a missing one fails the deploy before anything is written; README's secrets table names them.
