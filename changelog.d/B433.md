title: The box score shows both teams side by side on tablets and computers
---

- **On a tablet or computer the box score now shows both teams' batting at once, home team on the right, and its game details say "Time" and "Attendance" in full.** Phones still switch between the teams one at a time, and the inning-by-inning score at the top no longer carries a "Team" heading. Details: **B433** (#433) — `BoxRoom` draws both `BattingTable`s in a two-column grid at the `medium` and `desktop` pairings (away left, home right, each under its swatch and abbreviation) and keeps the switcher on `mobile`; `gameInfoLines` maps MLB's `T` / `Att` to `Time` / `Attendance`; `Linescore`'s team column header is `VisuallyHidden` "Team".
