- **Before, going to a date long ago meant tapping back one day at a time. Now, tapping the scoreboard's date opens a calendar:** type a date or pick one, jump to any month and year back to 1901 with your device's own pickers, and see how many games each day had; days without games are dimmed, and if you have favorite teams, a small red or yellow ✕ marks the regular-season days they don't play ("Off day: Yankees, Mets" on hover, or a press and hold on a phone). It slides up from the bottom on a phone and drops down under the date on larger screens, already filled in. Details: **S125** — `CalendarPanel` and the `date-selector` button in `DateControl` (shell-scoreboard A8), `useScheduleMonth` over S124's month route, `parseTypedDate`, S159's `favoriteTeamsOff` and S136's `monthGamesState`; adds `@mantine/dates` and `dayjs`.
