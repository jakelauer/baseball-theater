- **On a tablet or computer, Standings now show the American League and National League next to each other, so both leagues fit on one screen; phones still stack them.** Details: **B434** (#434) — `StandingsPage` groups the shown view's division tables by `leagueId` (in MLB's order) into one column per league, in a two-column grid at the medium and desktop layouts and one column on mobile; the clinch legend stays under both. The owner's words ("side by side on desktop and tablet", 2026-11-23, built without a design story) supersede the single-column Standings frame chosen in S38. Each table's sideways-scroll box (`DivisionTable`) is now a named, focusable region, so a keyboard can reach and scroll a table wider than its column.
