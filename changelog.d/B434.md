- **On a tablet or computer, Standings now show the American League and National League next to each other, so both leagues fit on one screen; phones still stack them.** Details: **B434** (#434) — `StandingsPage` groups the shown view's division tables by `leagueId` (in MLB's order) into one column per league, in a two-column grid at the medium and desktop layouts and one column on mobile; the clinch legend stays under both.
