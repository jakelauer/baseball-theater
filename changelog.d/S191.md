title: A favorite team's day off is marked only during that team's own season
---

- **Before, on the few days a year when one team's season had started early (an opening series in Seoul or Tokyo) or run late (a one-day tiebreaker), the coming calendar could have told you your favorite team had the day off even though its season hadn't begun or was already over. Now a favorite's day off counts only between that team's own first and last regular-season games.** When Baseball Theater doesn't hold enough of the year's schedule to tell, it keeps the earlier rule. Details: **S191** — each day of the month answer gains `regularSeasonTeamIds` (`dayRegularSeasonTeamIds`) and `inSeasonTeamIds` (`teamsInOwnSeason`, from the year's stored days), and `favoriteTeamsOff` counts only in-season favorites.
