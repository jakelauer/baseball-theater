title: A video keeps playing in a corner while you look around the site

- **Before, opening the box score, the plays or the scoreboard while a video played stopped it. Now the video keeps playing in a small player in the bottom-right corner while you browse, the way YouTube's mini-player works: pause or play it there, close it, or tap expand to go back to the page it came from with the full player.** A Mini player button under the video sends it to the corner without leaving the page, a paused video still closes when you leave, and opening another video replaces it. Details: **S198** — `PlayerProvider` in the shell holds the one `VideoPlayer` (keyed by the clip alone) and moves its holder node between `PlayerDialog`, `PlayerStage` and the new `CornerPlayer` with `appendChild`, so the same `<video>` element plays on with no reload; the corner sits above the update notices and leaves room after the footer so its Live strip stays clear; the player now loads as its own chunk once the shell mounts; built without a design under the owner's waiver of 2026-11-25 (S197 superseded).
