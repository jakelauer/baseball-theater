title: Rain delays and suspended games now say so
---

- **Before, a game stopped by rain or suspended looked exactly like one being played, with the same green "LIVE", and a game whose start was delayed looked like it would start on time. Now the game page and the scoreboard say "Delayed · Rain", "Delayed start · Rain" or "Suspended · Rain" in gold, so you can see at a glance that a game has stopped, or hasn't started, and why.** A game being played still shows its blinking green "LIVE", and its scoreboard row now reads the inning the way the design draws it ("Bot 7"). On a phone the longer labels sit on their own line under the team names. Details: **S170** — one `gameStatusMarker` in `packages/domain` sorts MLB's status into live, delayed, delayed start or suspended with MLB's reason; `LiveIndicator` draws it in `GameTopBar` and `ScoreboardModule` (game-shell A7), and `inningShortLabel` gives the row's inning.
