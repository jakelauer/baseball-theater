title: Every clip offers Auto quality in Chrome and Firefox too
---

- **Before, on desktop Chrome and Firefox the video player's quality picker left out MLB's adaptive "Auto" stream, so most clips offered only one or two qualities. Now every browser lists every quality MLB gives, Auto included, and if Auto ever fails to play, the clip carries on from the same moment in the standard file instead of stopping.** Details: **B415** (#415) — where `canPlayType` can't play HLS but Media Source Extensions can, `VideoPlayer` keeps the HLS quality in `playableQualities` and plays it through hls.js 1.7.4's light build, loaded with a dynamic `import()` only when an HLS quality plays (the entry bundle's budget is unchanged); the player is destroyed on a quality change, a new clip or closing, and a fatal hls.js error (or a chunk that won't load) switches to the same cut's MP4 (`fileQuality`) and drops HLS from the picker. Browsers that play HLS natively still do.
