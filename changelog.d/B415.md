title: The Auto quality comes to Chrome and Firefox
---

- **Before, on desktop Chrome and Firefox the video player's quality picker left out MLB's adaptive "Auto" stream, so most clips offered only one or two qualities. Now every browser lists every quality MLB gives for a clip, Auto included, and if Auto ever fails to play, the clip carries on from the same moment in the sharpest video file MLB gives for it instead of stopping.** Details: **B415** (#415) — where `canPlayType` can't play HLS but Media Source Extensions can, `VideoPlayer` keeps the HLS quality in `playableQualities` and plays it through hls.js 1.7.4's light build, loaded with a dynamic `import()` (`loadHls`) only when an HLS quality plays (the entry bundle's budget holds); the player is destroyed on a quality change, a new clip or closing, and a fatal hls.js error (or a chunk that won't load) drops HLS from the picker and switches, from the same moment, to the same cut's MP4 (`fileQuality`), else the clip's sharpest MP4; a clip with no MP4 at all has nothing to fall back to and shows Play. Browsers that play HLS natively still do.
