title: A game's room tabs share one row with its top bar
---

- **Before, a game page stacked its room tabs on a row under the back button and date. Now, the tabs sit in the same row, so the room you're in and the way back share one bar** (owner, 2026-11-24: "I'd prefer that to be all one row"; design waived 2026-11-25, "Build it without a design"). On a phone, where they can't all fit on one line, the tabs take a full-width line of their own inside the bar. Details: **B528** (#528) — `GameTopBar` takes the room tab list in its row; `GamePage` wraps the whole view in the rooms' `Tabs`.
