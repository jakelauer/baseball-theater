title: Suspended and forfeited games show the right state
---

- **Before, a suspended game looked like it had never started and a forfeited one never showed as over. Now a suspended game shows like a live one, so you can see what happened before play stopped, and is kept up to date for six hours after it was suspended; a forfeit counts as final.** A marker saying the game is suspended comes with its design later. Details: **B81** (#81, also fixing #79 and #80) — BT's game-status table now lists every `codedGameState` MLB's `GET /api/v1/gameStatus` sends (recorded in `fixtures/raw/gamestatus.json`), and a test fails as soon as MLB adds a code BT doesn't map or maps against MLB's group: suspended `T`/`U` are live and polled for `suspendedHours` (6) from when BT saw the status change (a new optional `GameSnapshot.suspendedAt`, #323; from first pitch when BT didn't see it), forfeits `Q`/`R` are final, a challenge or review (`M`/`N`) keeps its own letter, and BT's unknown is MLB's `X` rather than `U` (MLB's suspended code); `GameStatusCode` gains those letters (`pnpm api:breaking` 0, additive).
