title: Anonymous counts of the videos fans watch
---

- **Before, Baseball Theater had no idea which videos fans actually play or finish. Now, when you play a clip from a game's Videos tab, BT adds one to an anonymous daily count of how often that clip is started and how often it is watched to the end, with nothing about who watched, so a later feature can learn which clips to show first. The Privacy policy says so, and nothing on screen changes.** Details: **S174** — `POST /api/v1/clips/:mediaId/views` (204) into the `clip_engagement` table (per clip, game, UTC day, event and place in the ranked list; one atomic `count + 1`), sent once per opening by the Videos room's dialog through `navigator.sendBeacon` (`sendClipView`); a clip counts as finished at 90% played or at its end (`clipCompleted`); `pnpm clips:engagement-export --from --to` writes the counts as JSON lines for the ranking model (S185).
