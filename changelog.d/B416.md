title: The video player remembers your quality
---

- **Before, a quality picked in the video player applied to that clip only, and the next clip started back on Standard. Now the player remembers the quality you last chose, in this browser, signed in or out: the next clip starts in it, a clip that doesn't have it plays the nearest lower quality it does have (or the nearest higher, when there is none lower), and that stand-in never replaces your choice, so the next clip that has it plays it again.** Details: **B416** (#416) — `rememberedVideoQuality` (`packages/domain/src/video-quality.ts`) picks the clip's starting quality from the remembered one down a fallback ladder (Auto above its cut's files, so an unplayable Auto lands on the sharpest MP4 of its cut, where B415's hls.js failure already lands; the remembered cut, portrait or landscape, before the other); the settings store keeps the choice under its own non-personal key, `bt.videoQuality` (ADR-028), outside the account's settings, written only when the reader picks a quality.
