title: Switching plays no longer makes the page jump
---

- **Before, opening a play while another was open snapped the old one shut but slid the new one open, so the page jumped as you switched. Now the old play closes and the new one opens at the same instant, and its pitches start replaying straight away.** Details: **B521** (#521) — `PlaysAccordion` in `web/src/components/pitch/PlaysPanel.tsx` opens with a 0 ms transition when another at-bat was open at the tap, and starts its replay on the next tick rather than after the 600 ms fallback; opening a play from nothing, or closing one, still animates.
