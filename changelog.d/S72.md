title: Search narrows to a date, a team or a kind of play
---

- **Search can now be narrowed to one day, one team or one kind of play, such as home runs: each filter shows as a chip under the search box, and tapping its ✕ clears it and searches again.** Before, Search matched typed words only. Details: **S72** — `GET /api/v1/search/highlights` takes optional `date`, `team` and `tag` (additive; one parser, `parseSearchFilters`, for the route's 400 `invalid_filter` and the page), each sent to MLB as its own filter (`Date`, `TeamId`, `ContentTags`) so MLB's total counts every match; any filter switches MLB's query to `STRUCTURED`, the words then a `Description` clause (`mlbSearchQuery`, one rule in `packages/domain`), since MLB's free-text search ignores filters written into it; the page's "Active filters" row (`SearchFilterChips`) keeps the filters in the address; recorded `fixtures/search-team-147-tag-home-run.json`.
