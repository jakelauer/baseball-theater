title: A day's Best of appears noticeably faster the first time it is opened
---

- **Before, opening a day nobody had opened yet kept Best of loading for 7 to 10 seconds on a full slate, and the last games of that day could lose their rule settings for good. Now Best of waits only for the videos and plays it ranks, and each game's uniforms, rule settings, player of the game and play metrics are filled in just after. That first view may order a few clips without their play metrics; the next view orders them with them.** Details: **B577** (#577) — the digest's batch read (`readDayAndGames`) asks each cold game only its feed, content and GraphQL content, and the rest after the response (`askAfterResponse`, `storeLateAnswers`); in a simulated six-connection, 15-game day the answer moved from 5.9 s to 2.8 s.
