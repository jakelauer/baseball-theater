title: Videos played from Search or the scoreboard will link to their play, not just their game
---

- **Before, a highlight played from Search or the scoreboard's day digest could take you to its game but not to the play it shows, and the in-page video player linked to nothing. Now there is an approved design for every player: outside a game's own Videos page, a "More from game →" pill opens that game's videos, followed by the inning pill ("Top 6 →") that opens the play, and the in-page player shows the inning pill above the video. The app doesn't show it yet; the stories that build it come next.** Details: **S127** — standings-search-settings amendment A10, ratified by the owner 2026-11-22; both pills are instances of game-shell A6's `play-inning-pill` component; a clip with no play shows the game pill alone, a clip with no game no pill; the game pill's screen-reader name is "More videos from <game>".
