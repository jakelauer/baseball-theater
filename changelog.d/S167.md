title: The month's game counts also say which days are in the regular season
---

- **Before, Baseball Theater couldn't tell a regular-season day from a spring training, All-Star or postseason one when it showed a month, so the coming calendar would have marked your favorite team "off" on days that don't count, like the All-Star break or after its season ended. Now each day of the month says whether a regular-season game was played, so the calendar can mark a favorite team's day off only during the regular season.** Nothing on screen uses it yet: the calendar date picker comes next. Days saved before this change say "can't tell" until the next history fill re-saves them. Details: **S167** — each stored schedule game keeps MLB's `gameType`; each day of `GET /api/v1/schedule/month/:month` gains `regularSeason` (`dayRegularSeason` in `packages/domain/src/schedule-month.ts`, read by the same `listGameCounts` query; `null` for an unknown day or one stored without types), and `pnpm mlb:backfill` re-stores an untyped day once per run (`scheduleDayLacksGameType`).
