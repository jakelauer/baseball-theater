title: Faster live updates and Savant's video links are free for every fan
---

- **Before, Baseball Theater's account data marked faster live updates and Savant's play and pitch video links as perks for paying supporters. Now every fan has both, whatever their tier.** Both are built on MLB's data or video, which Baseball Theater doesn't sell. Nothing on screen changes: no feature was held back by either perk, and live games already send every update to every fan the moment it happens. Details: **S250** — `FREE_FOR_EVERY_FAN` in `packages/domain/src/entitlements.ts` sets `fasterLiveRefresh` and `savantLinks` to `true`; `entitlementsForTier` and `effectiveEntitlements` both apply it, so an account whose stored entitlements still say `false` is answered `true` by its session and `GET /api/v1/me` with nothing migrated; `fasterLiveRefresh` is kept only for compatibility; the OpenAPI spec (additive, `pnpm api:breaking` 0) and the web client's types describe both.
