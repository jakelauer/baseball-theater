title: A video's link to its play is now a small inning pill after the date

- **Before, a video of a play had an extra line under it, "Play · Top 6th →", linking to the play. Now the card's second line gives the date and then a small pill with the inning ("Top 6 →") that takes you to the play, while tapping anywhere else on the card still plays the clip; the video player shows the same pill, a screen reader names it "Open play - Top of 6th inning", and a clip with no play still says "Highlight".** Details: **S145** — game-shell A6 (S144): one `PlayInningPill` for the Videos room's cards and the floating player, the meta-line rule in `videoMeta`, `playInningLink` in `playLink.ts`, and the domain's `halfInningLabel` / `halfInningName` replacing `atBatInningLabel` (the Plays room's scoring tag calls it too).
