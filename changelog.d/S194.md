title: The calendar's months open straight from the cache and still pick up schedule changes within hours
---

- **Before, opening a month in the scoreboard's calendar could wait seconds while BT asked MLB for the whole month again, and a past year's months were fetched from BT's servers again every few hours or days. Now BT keeps this year's and next year's schedule fresh on its own, one month a minute, checking every month about every six hours, so the calendar's months come straight from the cache and still show schedule changes within hours; a past year's months are kept at the edge for as long as the cache allows.** Details: **S194** — a durable record per schedule month (`schedule_months`: when BT last checked it against MLB and when a refresh last claimed it) replaces each Worker isolate's in-memory one, so a new isolate no longer refetches every month (about 15 MB and a second of CPU each, #403, #410). The existing per-minute cron refreshes at most one due month a tick through the month route's own fill (at most 5 MLB requests a tick, 480 a day, fixed by the calendar rather than by visitors, ADR-002), and a fan's read asks MLB for a stored month only when that record says the refresh fell behind. The web asks for each month with a version from the new `GET /api/v1/schedule/month-versions/:year`, a hash of the month's answer, so a versioned month URL is edge-cached for 48 hours (a past year's for about 68 years, the longest the edge accepts) and a change reaches fans through a new URL within about ten minutes of the refresh storing it.
