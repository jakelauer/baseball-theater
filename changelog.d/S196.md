title: The video player opens as a framed window over the page

- **Before, a video played floating over a blacked-out page with its title underneath. Now it opens as a framed window with rounded corners over the dimmed page, which stays visible around it: the title (a little larger) sits on top, the browser's own controls on the clip (full screen, mute and volume among them), BT's quality and sharing tools underneath, and the MLB disclaimer at the bottom.** Close it with Close, Escape, or a click anywhere outside the window. Details: **S196** — `PlayerDialog` is an inset Mantine `Modal` (at most 90% of the viewport, `.bt-player-modal`, overlay at 70%) instead of a full-screen one; `VideoPlayer`'s `titleAbove` puts the title above the clip for the dialog, while the Videos room's stage keeps its layout; on a short screen, such as a phone on its side, only the clip shrinks so everything still fits; built from the site's existing dialogs under the owner's design waiver (S195 superseded), so S70's design captures now show this inset window rather than game-shell A4's ratified full-screen frame.
