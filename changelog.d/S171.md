title: Games that haven't started show their starting pitchers beside each team
---

- **Before, every game that hadn't started squeezed its probable starting pitchers into a small bordered box at the right of its scoreboard row. Now each starting pitcher sits beside his team, with the hand he throws with and his season record ("J. Steele (L)" over "11-5, 3.12"), and the box is gone, as the design draws it.** Fans who chose Room Menu still get its Videos, Plays and Box links on those games, and Hide Scores leaves the pitchers showing. Details: **S171** — `slotOccupant` gives `null` for Status before first pitch, `ScoreboardModule` draws each probable as `probableLabel` over `probableLine` in the row's body and keeps the box's space empty, and `ProbablePitcher` gains MLB's `pitchHand` (shell-scoreboard A16).
