title: The in-page video player links to the play it shows
---

- **Before, a fan watching in the in-page (stage) video player had no way to jump from the clip to the play it shows. Now a small inning pill ("Top 6 →") sits on its own row above the video, for the clip you chose or the first one waiting, and choosing it opens that play in the game's Plays page; a clip with no play shows no pill. Screen readers now name every inning pill starting with the words on screen, "Top 6: open play, top of 6th inning".** Details: **S172** — standings-search-settings A10: `PlayerStage` reuses S145's `PlayInningPill` and `videoPlayLink` for the shown clip; `playInningLink`'s spoken name starts with its visible words (WCAG 2.5.3).
