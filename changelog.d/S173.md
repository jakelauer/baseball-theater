title: Videos from Search and Best of link to their game and play
---

- **A video played from Search or the scoreboard's "Best of" list now shows two pills above it: `More from game →`, which opens that game's videos, and, for a clip of a play, its inning (`Bot 12 →`), which opens the play itself.** A clip with no play shows the game pill alone, and one tied to no game shows neither; the plain "Go to game" link is gone. Details: **S173** — `GET /api/v1/digest` gives each item its `play` (id and half-inning, through `atBatOfPlay`); `gameVideosLink` in `web/src/components/videos/playLink.ts` makes the game pill, drawn with S145's `PlayInningPill` beside the inning pill in `PlayerDialog`; Search passes `resultPlayLink`, the digest `playInningLink` over its item's play (standings-search-settings A10).
