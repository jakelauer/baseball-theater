title: Standings tables fill their column on wider screens

- **Before, on a desktop or tablet each division's standings table was only as wide as its text, a narrow block floating in its league's half of the page. Now, each table stretches across its column, the team names taking the extra room and the wins, losses and games back lined up at the right** (owner, 2026-11-25: "The standings should also not be floating mini columns on desktop; they should expand their widths."). Details: **B587** (#587) — `DivisionTable` draws its table at 100% width in every layout, the team cell taking the spare width over a 166px (192px with a clinched team) minimum; each table's keyboard-scrollable region and #563's breakpoints stay.
