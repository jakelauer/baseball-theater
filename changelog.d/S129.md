title: Standings will show spring training, the regular season and the playoffs
---

- **Before, nothing said how a fan would tell spring training's standings from the real ones, or get from one to the other. Now there is an approved design for Standings' three views: a switch under the heading between spring training, the regular season and the playoffs; "Standings as of March 2, 2027" on a day that isn't today; a gold box naming the World Series champions until January 31; and bold rows with MLB's clinch letters on the teams that made the playoffs. The app doesn't show it yet; S130 builds it.** Details: **S129** — standings-search-settings amendment A9, ratified by the owner 2026-11-19; its heading, World Series box, division tables, rows, clinch legend and spring training's first-morning block are now shared Figma components (`standings-heading`, `world-series-box`, `standings-division`, `standings-table-header`, `standings-row`, `clinch-legend`, `spring-empty`).
