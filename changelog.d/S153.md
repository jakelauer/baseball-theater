title: Search results jump straight to the play
---

- **A Search result for a play now shows its inning as a small pill after the date (`Bot 12 →`), and choosing it opens that play in the game's Plays room; choosing anywhere else on the row still plays the clip.** Results with no play keep their `Highlight · date` line. Details: **S153** — `GET /api/v1/search/highlights` returns each result's `playId` (MLB's `guid`), `halfInning` and `inning`; MLB's search types and zod parser moved to `packages/mlb-api` (#347); Search's rows reuse S145's `PlayInningPill`, `videoMeta` and `playInningLink`.
