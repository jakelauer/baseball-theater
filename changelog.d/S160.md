title: The standings data says how each team clinched
---

- **Before, the standings data said a team had clinched a playoff place but not how. Now each clinched team carries MLB's own letter for it: the best record in its league, its division, a wild card or a playoff spot, so the Standings page can mark those teams the way fans know from MLB's own tables.** Nothing on the page changes yet: the Standings views draw the letters and their legend. Details: **S160** — `GET /api/v1/standings` gains an optional `clinchIndicator` on every team row (MLB's letter as sent on a `clinched` team, `null` otherwise), mapped in `mapStandingsTeam` and carried by the top-level table and every phase's; additive (`pnpm api:breaking` 0), so an answer cached before it reads as no letter; S130 draws it.
