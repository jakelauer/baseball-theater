title: The box score shows the pitching and every detail
---

- **A game's box score now shows who pitched and how (innings, hits, runs, walks, strikeouts and ERA), and the details fans look for: doubles and home runs, RBIs, stolen bases, errors, double plays, the substitutes' notes and every game note MLB sends, laid out as the classic baseball.theater box score is.** Before, it stopped at the batting lines and a few game notes. Details: **S207** — `GameBoxscore` gains optional `pitching`, `details` and `notes` per team and richer batter lines, kept by `mapBoxscore` from the stored feed; one `StatTable` draws batting and pitching; Hide Scores masks only the new runs-bearing values; the differences from the live site, game by game, are in `docs/v3/design-checks/S207/v2-differences.md`.
