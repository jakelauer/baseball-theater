title: Auto-quality captions start off
---

- **Subtitles on the Auto (HLS) quality can now be turned off.** Before, Auto showed subtitles with no way to turn them off. On Safari and iPhone your system caption setting and the player's built-in CC menu now decide; on Chrome and Firefox Auto carries no captions at all. Details: **B580** (#580) — hls.js is started with `subtitleDisplay = false`, which states the intent only: the light build it loads has no subtitle support, so Chrome and Firefox get no caption track; Safari and iOS play HLS themselves, with the CC menu in the built-in controls (B579).
