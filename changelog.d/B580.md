title: Auto-quality captions start off

- **Captions on the Auto (HLS) quality start off, and the player's CC button turns them on when you want them.** Before, Auto showed subtitles with no way to turn them off. Details: **B580** (#580) — hls.js is started with `subtitleDisplay = false`; on Safari and iOS, which play HLS themselves, the system caption setting decides and the built-in controls (B579) carry the CC menu.
