title: Every game's Videos room shows the team filter
---

- **Every game's Videos room now shows the All / away / home team filter in its toolbar, instead of some games tucking it behind a Filters button.** Before, whether it showed depended on the teams' names, the clip counts and the screen's width; now it always shows, and on a narrow screen the room's other controls move to a second line to make room. Details: **B583** (#583) — `fittingFilters` always keeps the pinned quick filter, which the Videos room makes the Featured team control; `QuickFilters` holds a minimum width for it and the Filters button, and the Videos room's toolbar wraps; Stat / viz, and the Kind filter that replaces the team control when grouped by team, still fall back to the Filters drawer when they don't fit.
