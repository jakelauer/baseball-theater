title: Division names come from MLB
---

- **Before, Baseball Theater kept its own list of division names to label the standings. Now, each division's name ("AL East") comes straight from MLB with the standings, so the headings always match what MLB calls them.** The page looks the same. Details: **S102** — the standings requests hydrate `division`; the API's standings divisions gain optional `nameShort` and `abbreviation`; `divisionName` is gone.
