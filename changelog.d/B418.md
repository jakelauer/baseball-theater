- **Before, a game's Videos room always offered the Stat / viz filter, even when none of its clips was a stat or visualization clip, so choosing it emptied the list. Now the filter only shows when at least one clip fits it.** Details: **B418** (#418) — `VideosRoom` hides the Stat / viz pill and the Filters drawer's checkbox when `countHighlightFacets` (new, `packages/domain/src/highlights.ts`, counted with `classifyHighlight`) finds no stat / viz clip.
