title: Release notes no longer publish the owner's sign-off
---

- **The public list of release notes now carries only what changed in each release, not the owner's private sign-off on its notes.** Nothing had been published yet: no release has approved notes before v4.0.0. Details: **B327** (#327) — `GET /api/v1/releases` answers `PublicReleaseNotes` (`ReleaseNotes` without `approval`) for `current` and every entry in `releases`, stripped in one place in `getReleases`; `releases/<version>.json`, the bundle and `pnpm release:check` keep `approval`; the OpenAPI spec and web types are regenerated (`pnpm api:breaking` 0).
