title: Past days and finished games open straight from BT's own copy
---

- **Before, opening an earlier day's scores or a finished game could stall for most of a second (or many seconds for the day's best clips) while BT asked MLB again for data that hadn't changed. Now finished days and games come straight from BT's own copy, kept at Cloudflare's edge for up to 30 days, and the six-hourly refresh clears that copy only when something really changed, so past days open fast and still pick up rare corrections.** A finished game's full detail, late highlight clips included, is re-checked every six hours for a week after it was played. Clearing the edge copy waits on a Cloudflare purge token the owner adds; until then corrections show when the edge copy expires. Details: **S212** — `isPastFinishedDay` / `isPastFinishedGame` (`packages/domain/src/past-finished.ts`) decide past and finished; the schedule, game and digest reads skip MLB for such data and send `public, max-age=3600, s-maxage=2592000, stale-while-revalidate=86400`; `storeSchedule` / `storeGame` purge changed past URLs through the new `EdgeCachePurger` port (Cloudflare purge-by-URL, 30 URLs a request, a logged no-op without `BT_CF_PURGE_TOKEN`, `BT_CF_ZONE_ID` and `BT_APP_ORIGIN`); the cron's `recheckDueGame` re-ingests one recent final game a tick, recorded in the new `game_rechecks` table.
