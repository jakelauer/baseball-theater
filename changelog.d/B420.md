title: Plays open right away and close smoothly
---

- **Before, tapping a play in Plays could leave you waiting about a second before it opened, and closing one made the page jump. Now a play opens as soon as it has scrolled into place, and closing it slides shut.** Details: **B420** (#420) — `PlaysPanel`'s `afterScroll` waited for a `scrollend` the browser doesn't fire when nothing scrolls (or doesn't support), so it sat out its one-second fallback; it now also ends once the play reaches the top or stops moving for three frames. A closing play's contents were unmounted at once, so its panel collapsed empty; they now stay mounted until the collapse ends.
