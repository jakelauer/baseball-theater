title: A change log page shows what changed in each release
---

- **Before, there was nowhere in the app to see what a release changed. Now a "Changelog" link at the foot of the menu opens one page listing every release, newest first, in plain words: the big new features under a gold "Major Feature" header, each other change labelled as a feature, an enhancement, a performance change or a bug fix, and one line for the small fixes.** Anyone can open it, signed in or not. It starts at the public launch (v4.0.0); until that release's notes land it says "No release notes yet." Details: **S134**: `/changelog` (`ChangelogPage`, read through the `useReleases` hook on S132's `GET /api/v1/releases`), its loading, empty and error states through `ViewState`, and the menu's link in `NavRail` above the version line, plain or current (shell-scoreboard A9).
