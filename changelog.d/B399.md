title: The calendar loads a month's games in about two seconds, and never sticks on a blank month
---

- **Before, opening a month in the scoreboard's calendar could take 13–25 seconds, and when it took too long the month came back with no game labels at all, and stayed that way for minutes. Now a month BT hasn't loaded yet arrives in about two seconds, and a month BT couldn't fill is asked for again after a minute instead of being kept blank for five.** Details: **B399** (#399) — the month fill asked MLB for the whole month in one schedule request with the single-day hydrate, whose game-content part BT never reads: 20–29 MB and 10–15 s cold, which on staging came back with no days. It now asks week-long requests, at most five at a time, with only the hydrate the day mapping reads (~4 MB each, ~2 s for a month); a month with any unknown day is edge-cached for one minute (the fill's cooldown) with nothing stale after, instead of five; and the web client keeps such a month for one minute instead of five.
