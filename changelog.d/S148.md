- **Before, the scoreboard, game pages and Standings ended in "Fetched 12s ago · baseball.theater", which told a fan little. Now that line is gone, and a game page that is receiving live updates ends in a strip with a glowing, slowly blinking red dot and "Live Data · Active"; it disappears while the connection drops or falls back to polling, and holds still for anyone who prefers reduced motion.** Details: **S148** — shell-scoreboard A13 (S147): `SiteFooter` and `LiveMarker` in `web/src/layout/` replace `FooterMeta`; `LiveGameStream` reports when its socket is open and `useLiveGame` returns `{ inWindow, open }`.
