title: Refresh tests no longer risk failing around midnight
---

- **Contributors' checks can no longer fail by chance just before midnight US Eastern because of the refresh tests.** The refresh tests dated their game to MLB's today using the real clock, so a check that ran as the date turned over could see the game as already finished and fail for no fault of the change being checked. Details: **B642** (#642) — every case in `refresh.test.ts` now reads one pinned midday-Eastern clock (`vi.useFakeTimers({ toFake: ["Date"] })` in a `beforeEach`, with `STALE` / `FRESH` derived from it), extending B564's one-case pin to the whole file.
