title: An open pitch can now be closed, and a pitch you chose stays open when a new one arrives
---

- **Before, tapping an open pitch did nothing although its arrow said it would close, and in a live game every new pitch took over from whichever pitch you had opened. Now tapping an open pitch closes it, and a new pitch opens only when no pitch, or the last pitch thrown, is open; if you opened an earlier pitch, the new one arrives closed and yours stays open.** Details: **B522** (#522) — `PitchSequence` asks `onSelectPitch(null)` when its open row is tapped; `AtBatViewer` and `LiveRoom` keep "closed by the viewer" apart from "not chosen yet" (newest open), and the zone shows the open pitch or else the newest. `LiveRoom` replays an arrival only when it opens.
