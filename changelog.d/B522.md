title: An opened play shows every pitch closed, an open pitch can be closed, and a pitch you chose stays open when a new one arrives
---

- **Before, opening a play in Plays opened its last pitch for you, tapping an open pitch did nothing although its arrow said it would close, and in a live game every new pitch took over from whichever pitch you had opened. Now an opened play shows every pitch closed while its pitches still replay in the zone, tapping an open pitch closes it, and a new pitch opens only when no pitch, or the last pitch thrown, is open; if you opened an earlier pitch, the new one arrives closed and yours stays open.** Details: **B522** (#522) — `PitchSequence` asks `onSelectPitch(null)` when its open row is tapped; `AtBatViewer` starts with no pitch open and its opening replay opens none (the owner waived the Plays frame's open last pitch, 2026-11-25: "an opened play shows every pitch closed, its pitches still replay in the zone, and the zone shows the last pitch until you open one"); `LiveRoom` keeps "closed by the viewer" apart from "not chosen yet" (newest open) and replays an arrival only when it opens; the zone shows the open pitch or else the newest.
