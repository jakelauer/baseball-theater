title: The design for finding out what changed after updating on a phone's game page is approved
---

- **Before, a fan who updated the app while watching a game on a phone or tablet got no sign the update was done, because the "Update complete" pointer aimed at a menu button game pages don't have. Now the approved design shows a small "Update complete" notice there instead, which opens the change log in a window over the game, so the fan sees what changed without leaving it.** Details: **S192** — shell-scoreboard amendment A17, ratified by the owner 2026-11-23 ("Ratify as drawn"); S193 builds it.
