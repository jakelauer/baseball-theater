title: Faster first visits to a day or game
---

- **Before, opening a day's best clips, a game or a day that Baseball Theater hadn't stored yet made the server save everything and then read it all back, one step at a time, before answering, which could take nine seconds or more. Now, it answers from what it just fetched, reads a day's games together, and finishes saving after you have your page, so those first views open several seconds sooner; a slow play-by-play stats feed no longer holds a game page back either.** Details: **S216** — a cold 15-game digest went from 144 database round trips to 6 before its answer (8 in all); new `BackgroundWork` port (the Worker's `waitUntil`), batched `getMany` / `upsertMany` reads and writes, and a 750 ms limit on MLB's playMetrics, whose late answer is stored after the response.
