title: Search results look like every other video list, as a grid or a list
---

- **Search results now show each highlight the same way the rest of the site does, with its picture and length, and you can switch them between a grid and a list.** Before, Search drew its clips as plain text rows of its own, with no pictures and no choice of layout. Each result still shows its game's teams, its date and, when it has one, the inning button that opens its play. Details: **B588** (#588) — `SearchPage` draws each result as the shared `VideoCard` in `VideoGrid` and offers the Videos room's Grid / List control, now one component (`VideoLayoutToggle`) both use; `VideoGrid` takes a `layout` and stacks the cards as rows for List; `VideoCard` gains an optional `matchup` that starts its meta line (only Search passes it) and `testId` / `pillTestId` so Search keeps `search-result` / `search-play-link`; `SearchResultRow` is gone. A no-play result's `Highlight · date` now reads in `--bt-muted`, as on every other card, where game-shell A6 drew it in `--bt-light`.
