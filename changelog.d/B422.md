- **Before, a play with a video in Plays showed a "Video" label and gave you no way to watch the clip. Now that play's row has a play button that opens the clip in the player without expanding the play, and hovering it shows the clip's title.** Details: **B422** (#422) — `PlaysPanel` replaces game-rooms A6's `Video: <clip title>` line with a play button beside each at-bat's accordion toggle (not inside it, since the toggle is itself a button); its tooltip and accessible name are the clip's title (`highlightTitle`, so Hide Scores still masks the recap), and it opens the clip through the same `PlayerHost` the Videos room uses.
