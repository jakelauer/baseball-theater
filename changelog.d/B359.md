title: Games before 2005 are now stored in Baseball Theater
---

- **Before, filling Baseball Theater with past seasons stored almost no game from before 2005, and skipped the day of an All-Star Game whose schedule lists its sides without names. Now those games are stored like any other, and a game or day that still can't be stored says why in one line.** Details: **B359** (#359) — the live-feed parser accepted only numbers for a hit's spray-chart `coordinates.x`/`y`, which every pre-Statcast feed recorded sends as `null`, so `pnpm mlb:backfill` dropped each such game silently; the linescore lineup's `team` required a `name` the 2002 All-Star Game's sides lack, failing its whole schedule day. Both are now accepted (`null` coordinates, a nameless team; no reader downstream uses either), the fields the 1985/1995/2002 recordings added are modeled so `pnpm mlb:scan-drift` stays clean, and the backfill logs each failed feed's gamePk, and each failed schedule day, with the distinct field paths the parser rejected. Regression: `fixtures/raw/` recordings of 2002-07-09 (All-Star Game 12048), 1995-07-09 (209865) and 1985-07-07 (189236), each day trimmed to its one game.
