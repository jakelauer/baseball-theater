title: Games before 2005 can be downloaded into Baseball Theater
---

- **Before, filling Baseball Theater with past seasons stored almost no game from before 2005, and the All-Star Game and some spring and exhibition days of every year were skipped. Now those games are stored like any other, and a game that still can't be stored says why.** Details: **B359** (#359) — the live-feed parser accepted only numbers for a hit's spray-chart `coordinates.x`/`y`, which every pre-Statcast feed sends as `null`, so `pnpm mlb:backfill` dropped each such game silently; the linescore lineup's `team` required a `name` that All-Star and exhibition sides lack, failing their whole schedule day. Both are now optional (no reader downstream uses them), the fields the 1985/1995/2002 recordings added are modeled so `pnpm mlb:scan-drift` stays clean, and the backfill logs each failed feed's gamePk and rejected field paths. Regression: `fixtures/raw/` recordings of 2002-07-09 (All-Star Game 12048), 1995-07-09 (209865) and 1985-07-07 (189236), each day trimmed to its one game.
