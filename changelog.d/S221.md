title: A progress bar for backlog runs
---

- **Before, the owner had to read back through a backlog run's updates to tell how far it had got. Now every run prints a progress bar, the share of its planned stories and bugs finished with what's done, in progress, parked and next, after each merge or park and at the top of the run's summary; it reads the same in the terminal and the Claude app and stands out while scrolling.** Details: **S221** — `runs.ts` `runProgress` (each item's state from its newest item event; a `held` item leaves the total) and `runs.ts progress --journal <path>` (a plain-text 20-cell `█`/`░` bar), the run-level `plan` event (`claims.ts run event plan --items <ID>,…`), and run-backlog's SKILL.md saying when to log the plan and print the bar.
