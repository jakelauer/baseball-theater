title: After an update on a game page, phones and tablets see it and can read what changed without leaving the game
---

- **Before, updating the app while watching a game on a phone or tablet showed nothing: the "Update complete" pointer aimed at a menu button game pages don't have. Now a small "Update complete" notice shows at the bottom right of the game page, and tapping it opens the change log in a window over the game, which closes with its ✕, a tap outside it, Escape or the phone's Back button, leaving you on the game where you were.** It follows the pointer's rules: never with the update notice, gone after 10 seconds, and only once per release, even when you move between the game and another page while it shows. Details: **S193**: `UpdatedNotice` and `ChangelogModal` in `web/src/pwa/UpdatedNotice.tsx`, reusing the pointer's body and the change log page itself (shell-scoreboard A17).
