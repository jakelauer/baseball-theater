title: Every video is a link you can open in a new tab
---

- **Before, videos opened the player when clicked but weren't links, so a middle-click or "open in new tab" did nothing. Now every video is a link: open one in a new tab and you get the page you were on with that video playing, and closing it leaves you on that page.** A plain click still plays it in place. Details: **B424** (#424) — the open clip lives in the URL as `?clip=<id>` through one hook, `useOpenClip`; the Videos room's cards, Plays' play buttons, Search's rows and the day digest's rows are router links to it, and closing drops the param.
