title: Win probability on the scoreboard
---

- **Before, the scoreboard's data couldn't say how a game's odds had swung. Now, every live or finished game the scoreboard loads carries its win-probability line so far, ready for the scoreboard's Win Probability slot to draw without asking for each game separately; games saved before this change get their line too.** Details: **S161** — `scoreboardWinProbability` (built on the Live room's `winProbabilitySeries`) is worked out when a game's play metrics are written, stored on its `game_states` row and served as `winProbability` on each schedule game (null before first pitch); a one-time fill step gives older games theirs.
