- **Before, a game's video list was one narrow column of small rows, and a fan following one team had to filter clips team by team. Now, the list shows roomy rows in the style of the scoreboard's Best of, as many columns across as fit (one on a phone, up to three on a desktop), in a game's Videos tab and in Search alike, and one switch groups a game's clips by team, with a filter by kind (Pre-game, Game highlights, Statcast, Post-game) in place of the team filter.** Details: **S200** — `groupHighlightsByTeam` in the domain (away, home, then Other), a `Group by` control beside Grid / List, a Kind pill through `filterHighlights`, and `VideoCard`'s list row restyled as the Best of row in `VideoGrid`'s 1 / 2 / 3 list columns.
