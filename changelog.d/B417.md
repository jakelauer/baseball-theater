title: Share shows only where your device can share
---

- **Before, a video's Share button on a browser with no share sheet quietly copied the link, the same as Copy link beside it. Now Share appears only where your device can actually share the clip.** Details: **B417** (#417) — `PlayerActions` draws Share only when `navigator.share` exists and `navigator.canShare` (where the browser has it) accepts the clip's title and URL; the copy fallback is gone.
