title: Player names open their stats
---

- **Before, a fan who wanted a player's stats had to leave Baseball Theater and search for the player. Now, a player's name on a play's pitcher and batter cards (in the Plays and Live rooms) and in the box score opens that player's stats page in a new tab, on MLB.com by default; signed-in fans see a Stats Site choice in Settings, and patrons can pick FanGraphs, Baseball-Reference or Baseball Savant instead.** Details: **S209** — one `PlayerLink` component and one `playerProfileUrl` function; FanGraphs and Baseball-Reference ids come from the Chadwick Bureau's open player register, with MLB's own cross-references filling the ids it lacks (such as this season's rookies' FanGraphs ids), preseeded into `player_ids` and kept in step by the cron's daily per-file sync, which downloads a register file only when its ETag has changed, and served for many players at once by `GET /api/v1/players/profiles`; the choice is `statsProvider` in the account's settings, open to every patron tier (Backer and up).
