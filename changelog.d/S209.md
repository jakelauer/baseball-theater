title: Player names open their stats
---

- **Before, a fan who wanted a player's stats had to leave Baseball Theater and search for the player. Now a player's name on a play's pitcher and batter cards (in the Plays and Live rooms) and in the box score opens that player's stats page in a new tab, on MLB.com by default; signed-in fans see a Stats Site choice in Settings, and patrons can pick FanGraphs, Baseball-Reference or Baseball Savant instead.** Details: **S209** — one `PlayerLink` component and one `playerProfileUrl` function; FanGraphs and Baseball-Reference ids come from the Chadwick Bureau's open player register (MLB's cross-references carry no Baseball-Reference ids), stored in `player_ids` and looked up a capped batch at a time by the cron, and served by `GET /api/v1/players/:personId/profiles`; the choice is `statsProvider` in the account's settings, open to every patron tier (Backer and up).
