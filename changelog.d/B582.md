title: A video link opened in a new tab no longer opens the player with a stray red box
---

- **Before, opening a video's link in a new tab (most visibly on a phone) opened the player with a red outline around its top-left link or its Close button, as if something were wrong. Now it opens clean, the same as tapping the video in place.** Details: **B582** (#582) — `PlayerDialog` gives its opening focus to Mantine's hidden `FocusTrap.InitialFocus` instead of its first control; on a page the reader hasn't touched yet, the browser treated that focus as keyboard focus and drew its ring. Tab, Esc and Space still work as before.
