title: Baseball Theater can now tell which of your favorite teams have the day off
---

- **Before, nothing worked out which of your favorite teams have a day off, only whether one of them plays. Now one tested rule says, for each regular-season day of a month, which of your favorites don't play and whether the coming calendar should mark the day red (none of them plays) or yellow (some do).** It leaves out a favorite with no games that month (a team that didn't exist yet, or whose season is over) and never marks spring training, All-Star or postseason-only days. Nothing on screen uses it yet: the calendar date picker draws the mark next. Details: **S159** — `favoriteTeamsOff` in `packages/domain/src/schedule-month.ts` replaces `dayHasFavoriteTeam`, reading each month day's `teamIds` (S136) and `regularSeason` (S167).
