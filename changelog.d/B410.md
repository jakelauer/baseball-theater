title: A calendar month BT has just loaded shows its games straight away, not a blank month
---

- **Before, the first time anyone opened a month in the scoreboard's calendar, BT loaded its games but could still answer with no game labels on any day, and kept answering that way for about a minute. Now the month comes back with its games the first time. Opening a month BT hasn't loaded yet still takes several seconds; that is BT saving the month one day at a time, which is being reworked separately.** Details: **B410** (#410) — after a fill, the month route read the month's counts again, the same database query as its first read; with Hyperdrive's query cache on (#408) that read returned the pre-fill answer, every filled day unknown (seen on staging: 2027-06 answered 0 of 30 days known after a 9.2 s fill, then all 30 a minute later). The fill now answers from the days it just stored, laid over the first read (`scheduleDayTally`, now the one tally the memory repository uses too), which also saves a round trip. The rest of a cold month's 8–11 s is about 62 database round trips one after another (each day's read and write through Hyperdrive, ~70–200 ms each), to be batched with S150's rewrite of the schedule writes.
