title: An approved design for picking any date from a calendar
---

- **Before, the only way to reach a date long ago on the scoreboard was to tap back one day at a time. Now there is an approved design for a calendar that opens from the date: type a date or pick one, switch the month and year with your device's own pickers, see the weekdays and each day's game count, and spot the days your favorite teams are off. The app doesn't show it yet; S125 builds it.** Details: **S123** — shell-scoreboard amendment A8, ratified by the owner 2026-11-19; the date control and every part of the calendar are now shared Figma components (`date-selector`, `calendar-panel`, `calendar-day` and their parts).
