title: Hover lifts and zooms glide instead of juddering
---

- **Before, a game card's lift and a clip thumbnail's zoom could judder as you hovered them, and the "Update complete" countdown line stepped down a pixel at a time. Now they glide smoothly.** The hover movements keep their own compositor layer, so they move in fractions of a pixel, and the countdown shrinks by scaling instead of changing its width; tests keep every hover transition off position and size properties. Details: **B413** (#413) — `will-change: transform` on `.bt-card`, `.bt-zoom img` and `.bt-pitch-dot circle` in `web/src/styles.css`; the after-update pointer's `Countdown` is a `scaleX` fill instead of Mantine's width-animated `Progress`.
