title: Fans will be able to see what changed in each update
---

- **Before, nothing showed what a change log page or the update notices would look like, so there was no way to tell fans what an update brought. Now there is an approved design: a change log page at `/changelog` listing each release's highlights under a gold "Major Feature" header, labelled feature, enhancement, performance or bug fix, with smaller fixes on one line; a "Changelog" link at the foot of the menu; a "See the changelog" link on the update notice; and, after an update, a small pointer at that link for ten seconds. The app doesn't show it yet; S134 and S135 build it.** Details: **S133** — shell-scoreboard amendment A9, ratified by the owner 2026-11-19; the link's words became one word, "Changelog", at ratification, and its frames' shared pieces (rail, drawer, update notice, the change log page's parts) are now Figma components.
