title: Standings on any date, with the scoreboard's calendar
---

- **Before, seeing the standings on a past date meant editing the address by hand. Now Standings has the same date bar and calendar as the scoreboard: pick a day, type one, or step a day at a time, and see the standings as they were then; Today brings back the current standings (or, in the off-season, the season's final table with the World Series box).** Details: **S138** — `StandingsPage` mounts S125's `DateControl` first under the header, sending a pick, a typed date or an arrow to `/standings?date=<day>` (dropping `?view=`, so the day opens its own first view) and `Today` to `/standings`; the control and the "as of" words read one check (a real date from 1901-04-18 on, `BACKFILL_EARLIEST_DATE`), so `?date=1850-01-01` reads as today in both; `DateControl` and `CalendarPanel` take an optional `onToday` for a page whose today isn't a day.
