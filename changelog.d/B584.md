title: Loading pages keep their navigation and look like what is coming
---

- **Before, every page loaded behind the same grey bars, and some loads hid the navigation: a game's page lost its whole top bar and room tabs until the game arrived, and the scoreboard's heading changed when its games landed. Now, each page's placeholder broadly takes the shape of its content (game cards on the scoreboard, tables on Standings, result rows on Search, the highlight grid, play rows and box tables in a game's rooms), and the top bar, room tabs, Back, date controls and headings show at once and stay put when the data arrives.** Details: **B584** (#584) — `ViewState`'s loading takes a `SkeletonShape` (`games`, `videos`, `table`, `results`, `text`, `rows`), following NN/g's, Atlassian's and Carbon's skeleton guidance; `GamePage` draws `GameTopBar` (now taking `game | null`, `aria-busy` while loading) and the room tabs from a cached day's listing (`findScheduledGame`) or skeletons before the game answers; the scoreboard keeps its `Games — <date>` heading and, undated, the date bar (`DateControlSkeleton`) while loading.
