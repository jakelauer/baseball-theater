title: Every release's change log is approved by the owner before it goes out
---

- **Before, a release could reach fans with no summary of what changed, and nobody had signed off on how its changes were described. Now, from the public launch (v4.0.0) on, every release carries one short list for fans (its big additions, each other notable change as a feature, an enhancement, a performance change or a bug fix, and one line for the small fixes), the owner approves that list before it ships, and production refuses to deploy a release without it.** Nothing fans see changes yet: the change log page comes with later work. Details: **S131** — `ReleaseNotes` and `parseReleaseNotes`, `parseReleaseVersion`, `compareReleaseVersions` and `currentRelease` in `packages/domain/src/release-notes.ts`; `pnpm release:check <version>` (`functions/src/scripts/release-check.ts`), run by the production deploy job before it builds; the release rule in `CLAUDE.md` drafts `releases/<version>.json` and quotes the owner's approval.
