title: Pages show a pulsing outline of what's coming while they load
---

- **Before, a page that was still loading showed a spinning circle. Now it shows a softly pulsing outline of what's coming: the scoreboard draws placeholder game cards, and other pages draw placeholder rows.** Details: **B431** (#431) — `ViewState`'s `loading` draws Mantine `Skeleton`s (animated) in place of the `Loader`, card blocks when given `cards` (the scoreboard's columns, `scoreboardColumns`) and rows otherwise. Every page and room that loads through `ViewState` follows: the scoreboard, standings, search, change log, the game page, the sign-in callback and the route-level fallback.
