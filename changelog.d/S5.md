- **The Search page now finds real highlight videos: type a few words, like "walk-off", and get MLB's matching clips, newest first, each with its date and the two teams, and tap one to watch it with a link to its game.** Before, Search was an empty placeholder. Details: **S5** — `GET /api/v1/search/highlights?q=` asks MLB's free-text search (`fastball-gateway.mlb.com`, `queryType: FREETEXT`, hyphens sent as spaces) per search, server-side (ADR-029), through a new `MlbStatsClient.searchHighlights`; local and CI answer from the recorded `fixtures/search-walk-off.json`; the page, "Search Highlights", lists typographic rows (`Highlight · date`, `MatchupAbbr`) and plays a result in S70's floating player; the request also asks for each clip's `guid` and `playInfo` for S153.
