title: The video player opens smoothly, then plays

- **Before, the video player popped onto the screen all at once and started playing, sound and all, while it appeared. Now it opens with a very brief fade and scale, and the clip starts only once it is open.** With reduced motion on, it opens and plays at once. Details: **B423** (#423) — `PlayerDialog` mounts closed and opens a frame later so Mantine's 150ms `pop` transition runs, and keys `VideoPlayer` on the transition's end so `autoPlay` turns on only then.
