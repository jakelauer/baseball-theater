Now it keeps each park's field outline from Baseball Savant — fetched once, the first time a game there comes in, and cleaned of anything that could run or load from elsewhere — and serves it itself, so a future spray chart can show where the balls went without anyone's browser asking Savant.** Details: **S105** — `MlbStatsClient.fetchVenueField`, kept by `keepVenueFields` after `sanitizeFieldSvg` in the `venue_fields` table, served at `GET /api/v1/venues/:venueId/field.svg` (`image/svg+xml`, cached a day in the browser and 30 days at the edge); recorded as `fixtures/raw/savant-field-4169.svg` and `fixtures/raw/savant-field-3313.svg`.
