title: Favorites and live games first on the scoreboard, with a sort choice
---

- **Before, the scoreboard listed the day's games in the order MLB sent them, so a postponed game could sit above one being played. Now your favorite teams' games come first, then games in progress, then the rest from earliest to latest, and a Sort menu beside the day's heading switches to plain start-time order.** The choice resets to the default on your next visit. Details: **B430** (#430) — one domain function, `sortScoreboardGames` (favorites by `isFavoriteGame`, in progress by `gamePhase`), orders the games before `scoreboardRows`; the control reuses the Videos room's sort pill.
