title: The footer no longer floats halfway up a short page
---

- **Before, on a page with little on it the footer could sit in the middle of the screen with empty space below it. Now the page's content area fills the window, so the footer stays at the bottom of the window on a short page and still follows the content on a long one.** The owner: "It's never pinned, but the content's minimum height should be either a percentage of the viewport height or something similar to avoid a highwater footer". Details: **B525** (#525) — the shell (`.bt-shell`) is at least `100dvh` tall (after a `100vh` fallback) and `<main>` grows into the leftover height; the footer itself is never pinned.
