- **Before, most pages simply stopped, with no footer at all. Now every page ends in the same footer: links to the Changelog and to Support & Community (the r/BaseballTheater subreddit, in a new tab), a "Your data" link to Settings for signed-in fans, and "© 2026 Baseball Theater" below them; on a live game page it still opens with the blinking "Live Data · Active" strip.** Details: **S142** — shell-scoreboard A14 (S141): `SiteFooter` grown to hold the link columns and drawn by `AppShellLayout` after `<main>` (not by pages, not on the account-deleted page or in the nav drawer); pages tell it about their live socket through the render prop's `setFooter` (`useFooterMode`); the year comes from `copyrightYear` in `packages/domain`. About, Privacy policy and Terms of use links come with S154's pages.
