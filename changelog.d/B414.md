title: Esc closes the video player on the stage too
---

- **Before, readers who chose the Stage video player in Settings could press Esc and nothing happened: only the pop-up player closed on Esc. Now Esc closes a clip playing on the stage, as it does the pop-up, and puts focus back on the clip card you pressed. In full screen the first Esc leaves full screen and a second one closes the clip; with the quality menu open, Esc closes the menu first.** Details: **B414** (#414) — `PlayerStage` had no Escape handler; it now listens for Esc while a chosen clip plays and calls `PlayerHost`'s `onClose`, skipping a press that full screen, an open menu or another handler already took, and keys its `VideoPlayer` on whether the clip was chosen so closing goes back to the waiting first clip.
