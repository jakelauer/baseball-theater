title: Esc closes the video player on the stage too
---

- **Before, readers who chose the Stage video player in Settings could press Esc and nothing happened: only the pop-up player closed on Esc. Now Esc closes a clip playing on the stage, as it does the pop-up, and puts focus back on the clip card you pressed, unless you have moved on to something else on the page, where focus stays. In full screen the first Esc leaves full screen and a second one closes the clip; with a menu or the Filters panel open, Esc closes that and the clip keeps playing.** Details: **B414** (#414) — `PlayerStage` had no Escape handler; it now listens for Esc while a chosen clip plays and calls `PlayerHost`'s `onClose`, skipping a press that full screen, an open menu, a dialog or drawer, or another handler already took, returns focus to the card only from the page or the player itself, and keys its `VideoPlayer` on whether the clip was chosen so closing goes back to the waiting first clip.
