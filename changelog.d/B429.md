title: Three games per row on wide screens
---

- **Before, the scoreboard showed at most two games per row, however wide the screen. Now it fits three on a wide screen, then two, then one as the window narrows.** Details: **B429** (#429) — `scoreboardColumns` in `ScoreboardPage` gives desktop three columns (medium two, mobile one), and the loading skeleton follows it.
