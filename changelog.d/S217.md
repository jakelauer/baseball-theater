title: Leaner Best of
---

- **Before, Baseball Theater's server still worked out and sent a "watch first" mark for each clip in a day's Best of, though the site stopped showing that label when Best of moved to the highlight grid. Now, the day's Best of carries only what the site uses, so there is less to compute and send.** Details: **S217** — `DigestItem.watchFirst` is gone from `dayDigest`, the `/api/v1/digest` OpenAPI spec and the generated web client; a clip's place is its `rank`.
