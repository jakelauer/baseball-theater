**Gap:** BT is to credit MLB in two more ways: a line in the site footer saying the data and video are © MLB Advanced Media and that BT is unaffiliated, and each clip's visible MLB credit with a link to MLB's own page for it. Neither is built: the footer (`web/src/layout/SiteFooter.tsx`) ends in "© {year} Baseball Theater"; the player (`VideoPlayer`) shows only its "Disclaimer:" line; and BT doesn't keep a link to MLB's page for a clip. S252 was to draw both first; the owner said it is not needed (2026-11-28: *"S252 needs no design"*) and the coordinator closed it as superseded on those words, so this story builds them into the existing components with no design (the exemption below).

**Gap (from S229, merged in):** S209 (merged in pull request #677) builds each player's FanGraphs and Baseball-Reference profile links from the Chadwick Bureau's open player register (`functions/src/adapters/http/chadwick-register.ts`, preseeded and synced daily into `player_ids`). The register's README (`chadwickbureau/register`, read at its commit `ba48ce1`, 2026-11-01) says the dataset "is made available under the Open Data Commons Attribution License", which asks anyone who publicly uses the database, or a work produced from it, to say where the data came from. Nothing on the site says so: the About page's "Where the data comes from" section (`web/src/pages/legal/about.md`, words the owner approved on 2026-11-23 under S154) names only Major League Baseball's feeds. MLB's content feed gives each clip a `slug` (typed in `packages/mlb-api/src/content.ts` and `graphql-content.ts`; e.g. the clip `conforto-clubs-cubs-200th-homer-in-close-win` in `fixtures/raw/content-823823.json`'s `highlights.highlights.items[]`; in that recording each of the 40 clips' `id` equals its `slug`, and every slug matches `[a-z0-9-]+`), from which MLB's video page is expected to be `https://www.mlb.com/video/<slug>` (unverified until AC 1's check), but the mapper (`functions/src/mappers/content.ts`) drops it, so `MediaHighlight` has no such field; Search's answer carries no slug (`fixtures/search--team-147--tag-home-run.json`). A legal research note the owner read on 2026-11-28 (research, not legal advice) suggested these changes as goodwill that also removes any claim BT is confusable with MLB; the owner: *"I want to do all your suggestions except redrawing the fields (which I'll do but not yet)."* (2026-11-28).

**Impact:** Before S253, a fan could read BT's pages without being told the games' data and video belong to MLB, and had no way to open a clip on MLB's own site. After S253, every page's footer says so, and every clip names MLB and links to its page on MLB.com where MLB has one. And where BT used a free player register whose licence asks for credit, and gave none, the About page now says where the player ids come from and under what licence, as the licence asks.
