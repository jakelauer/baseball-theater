**Gap:** BT's plan for supporters is v2's: Patreon's four cumulative tiers (`Backer` → `Pro Backer` → `Star Backer` → `Premium Sponsor`, `PatronTier` in `packages/domain/src/types.ts`) unlocking features through server-side entitlements (`entitlementsForTier`, `packages/domain/src/entitlements.ts`), with a staging-only tier override for tests (S215), the next-pitch odds planned for "the paid tier" (S190, S91), what the AI writes planned for patrons only (FEATURES' AI game companion row, S178), and S209's stats-site choice open to Backer and up. On 2026-11-28 the owner read a legal research note on BT's use of MLB's data and video (research, not legal advice), which found that calling support a donation doesn't hold once support unlocks features: tax law treats a gift as "detached and disinterested generosity", a tier that unlocks features is a purchase (Patreon itself collects sales tax on benefits), and copyright's commercial-use question asks whether the user profits from the material without paying the customary price; MLB's notice (`http://gdx.mlb.com/components/copyright.txt`) permits only *"individual, non-commercial, non-bulk use"*. What does help, it said, is a pure tip jar with no perks, or perks that are only BT's own work; it suggested moving faster live refresh and the Savant video links to free and charging for BT's own work, naming the AI companion, the next-pitch model, push and curation. S250 makes those two MLB-sourced perks free. The owner then said, the same day: *"Actually... if it makes no difference that Patreon acts like a donation then I probably want to phase out Patreon in favor of Link, Stripe, or similar"*; *"I probably will want to just retire all Patreon tiers, but include the patreon connector to figure out what backer level the person was and give them a great deal or an automatic backer status on the new tiers. I think the new tiers will include entirely different perks for legal reasons, and I'll want to do a thorough check on what those features could be"*; and then *"I'm not sure I even want tiers"*. So how supporters fund BT, and what, if anything, they get, is open, and the provider and the former-backer benefit (S259) turn on it.

The provider is open too. BT's architecture keeps supporters' payments on Patreon: ADR-004 (as amended by ADR-019, `docs/v3/ARCHITECTURE.md`) keeps payments and membership funding on Patreon, with Patreon linked by OAuth and its webhooks setting entitlements. The same note found that Patreon's framing of support as a donation gives BT no protection once support unlocks anything, and the owner, after the first quote above, said *"Compare the cut taken by patron to the ones that do the tax work for you"* (same day). Nothing records which provider, at what cost against Patreon's, or who handles sales tax and VAT. The choices differ in kind: with plain Stripe (Stripe Billing for subscriptions, Stripe Tax to calculate tax), BT is the merchant of record and must register, collect and file sales tax and VAT itself, and a seller outside the EU owes EU VAT on digital services from its first sale to an EU consumer; a merchant-of-record service (Paddle, Polar, Stripe Managed Payments; Lemon Squeezy is being folded into Stripe Managed Payments) sells on BT's behalf and handles that tax for a larger cut. At small amounts a per-payment fixed fee dominates the cost, so annual billing changes the comparison. (Each of these facts is to be checked on its owner's own page in the story, AC 7 and 8.) Which provider fits depends on how supporters fund BT: if the owner chooses no perks, one-off or recurring support with nothing unlocked, a provider that can take tips is enough; a membership or tiers needs subscriptions whose state BT reads.

**Impact:** Before S256, BT was set to copy v2's paid tiers without anyone having asked whether it should have tiers at all, and the owner's probable move off Patreon named no replacement and no cost. After S256, the owner has chosen, from a written comparison, between a pure tip jar with nothing unlocked, one supporter membership, or several tiers, and what supporters get, before anything about payments is built; and then picked a payment provider from a dated comparison of fees against Patreon's at BT's own price points, tax handling, refunds, payouts and fit with BT's Cloudflare servers, and the architecture records the choice as a proposed amendment for the owner to accept.
