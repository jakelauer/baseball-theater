**Gap:** BT is to credit MLB in two more ways: a line in the site footer saying the data and video are © MLB Advanced Media and that BT is unaffiliated, and each clip's visible MLB credit with a link to MLB's own page for it. Neither is built: the footer (`web/src/layout/SiteFooter.tsx`) ends in "© {year} Baseball Theater"; the player (`VideoPlayer`) shows only its "Disclaimer:" line; and BT doesn't keep a link to MLB's page for a clip. S252 was to draw both first; the owner closed it as superseded (2026-11-28: *"S252 needs no design"*), so this story builds them into the existing components with no design (the exemption below). MLB's content feed gives each clip a `slug` (typed in `packages/mlb-api/src/content.ts` and `graphql-content.ts`; e.g. the clip `conforto-clubs-cubs-200th-homer-in-close-win` in `fixtures/raw/content-823823.json`'s `highlights.highlights.items[]`; in that recording each of the 40 clips' `id` equals its `slug`, and every slug matches `[a-z0-9-]+`), from which MLB's video page is expected to be `https://www.mlb.com/video/<slug>` (unverified until AC 1's check), but the mapper (`functions/src/mappers/content.ts`) drops it, so `MediaHighlight` has no such field; Search's answer carries no slug (`fixtures/search--team-147--tag-home-run.json`). A legal research note the owner read on 2026-11-28 (research, not legal advice) suggested these changes as goodwill that also removes any claim BT is confusable with MLB; the owner: *"I want to do all your suggestions except redrawing the fields (which I'll do but not yet)."* (2026-11-28).

**Impact:** Before S253, a fan could read BT's pages without being told the games' data and video belong to MLB, and had no way to open a clip on MLB's own site. After S253, every page's footer says so, and every clip names MLB and links to its page on MLB.com where MLB has one.
