**Gap:** BT's supporters pay on Patreon, and BT's architecture says so: ADR-004 (as amended by ADR-019, `docs/v3/ARCHITECTURE.md`) keeps payments and membership funding on Patreon, with Patreon linked by OAuth and its webhooks setting entitlements. On 2026-11-28 the owner read a legal research note (research, not legal advice) which found that Patreon's framing of support as a donation gives BT no protection once support unlocks anything, and said: *"Actually... if it makes no difference that Patreon acts like a donation then I probably want to phase out Patreon in favor of Link, Stripe, or similar"* (owner, 2026-11-28), and then *"Compare the cut taken by patron to the ones that do the tax work for you"* (same day). Nothing records which provider, at what cost against Patreon's, or who handles sales tax and VAT. The choices differ in kind: with plain Stripe (Stripe Billing for subscriptions, Stripe Tax to calculate tax), BT is the merchant of record and must register, collect and file sales tax and VAT itself, and a seller outside the EU owes EU VAT on digital services from its first sale to an EU consumer; a merchant-of-record service (Paddle, Polar, Stripe Managed Payments; Lemon Squeezy is being folded into Stripe Managed Payments) sells on BT's behalf and handles that tax for a larger cut. At small amounts a per-payment fixed fee dominates the cost, so annual billing changes the comparison. (Each of these facts is to be checked on its owner's own page in the story, AC 2 and 3.) Which provider fits depends only loosely on how supporters fund BT (S256): if the owner chooses no perks, one-off or recurring support with nothing unlocked, a provider that can take tips is enough; a membership or tiers needs subscriptions whose state BT reads.

**Impact:** Before S257, the owner's probable move off Patreon named no replacement and no cost. After S257, the owner has picked a payment provider from a dated comparison of fees against Patreon's at BT's own price points, tax handling, refunds, payouts and fit with BT's Cloudflare servers, and the architecture records the choice as a proposed amendment for the owner to accept.
