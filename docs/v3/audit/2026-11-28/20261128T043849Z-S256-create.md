**Gap:** BT's plan for supporters is v2's: Patreon's four cumulative tiers (`Backer` → `Pro Backer` → `Star Backer` → `Premium Sponsor`, `PatronTier` in `packages/domain/src/types.ts`) unlocking features through server-side entitlements (`entitlementsForTier`, `packages/domain/src/entitlements.ts`), with a staging-only tier override for tests (S215), the next-pitch odds planned for "the paid tier" (S190, S91), what the AI writes planned for patrons only (FEATURES' AI game companion row, S178), and S209's stats-site choice open to Backer and up. On 2026-11-28 the owner read a legal research note on BT's use of MLB's data and video (research, not legal advice), which found that calling support a donation doesn't hold once support unlocks features: tax law treats a gift as "detached and disinterested generosity", a tier that unlocks features is a purchase (Patreon itself collects sales tax on benefits), and copyright's commercial-use question asks whether the user profits from the material without paying the customary price; MLB's notice (`http://gdx.mlb.com/components/copyright.txt`) permits only *"individual, non-commercial, non-bulk use"*. What does help, it said, is a pure tip jar with no perks, or perks that are only BT's own work; it suggested moving faster live refresh and the Savant video links to free and charging for BT's own work, naming the AI companion, the next-pitch model, push and curation. S250 makes those two MLB-sourced perks free. The owner then said, the same day: *"Actually... if it makes no difference that Patreon acts like a donation then I probably want to phase out Patreon in favor of Link, Stripe, or similar"*; *"I probably will want to just retire all Patreon tiers, but include the patreon connector to figure out what backer level the person was and give them a great deal or an automatic backer status on the new tiers. I think the new tiers will include entirely different perks for legal reasons, and I'll want to do a thorough check on what those features could be"*; and then *"I'm not sure I even want tiers"*. So how supporters fund BT, and what, if anything, they get, is open, and the provider (S257) and the former-backer benefit (S259) turn on it.

**Impact:** Before S256, BT was set to copy v2's paid tiers without anyone having asked whether it should have tiers at all. After S256, the owner has chosen, from a written comparison, between a pure tip jar with nothing unlocked, one supporter membership, or several tiers, and what supporters get, before anything about payments is built.
