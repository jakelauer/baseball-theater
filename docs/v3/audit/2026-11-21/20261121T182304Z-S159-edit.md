**Gap:** S123's ratified calendar (shell-scoreboard A8, "Favorite teams' off-day mark", `A8.md:81`) marks a day some or all of the fan's favorite teams don't play: a red ✕ when none of them plays, a yellow ✕ when at least one plays but not all, and a tooltip naming those off ("Off day: Yankees, Mets"). S125 AC 2 reads them through "S136's domain function that lists the favorites off that day", but S136 shipped only `dayHasFavoriteTeam` (`packages/domain/src/schedule-month.ts:89`), a yes/no that answers the reverse question (does a favorite play), which nothing outside its tests calls. **Impact:** Before S159, nothing worked out which of a fan's teams have the day off. S159 impact: one tested function says which favorites are off each day and whether the calendar marks the day red or yellow, ready for the calendar (S125) to draw.

**Acceptance criteria** (checks `scripts/verify-S159.sh` performs)

1. `favoriteTeamsOff(teamIds, favoriteTeamIds, monthDays)` in `packages/domain/src/schedule-month.ts`, exported from `@bt/domain`, where `monthDays` is the shown month's days as S124 serves them (`readonly Pick<ScheduleDayCount, "teamIds">[]`). **Only the favorites with games that month count:** a favorite whose ID is in no day's `teamIds` in `monthDays` is left out of the mark and of `off`, so a team that didn't exist yet, or is out of the postseason, never marks a day (owner, 2026-11-21, asked "When a favorite team has no games at all in the month the calendar shows, should its days still be marked as off days?": chose *"Only teams with games that month (Recommended)"*: "a favorite that plays no game in the shown month is left out of the mark and the tooltip, so a team that didn't exist yet, or is out of the postseason, never marks a day."). A day whose `teamIds` is `null` or missing names no team, so it never puts a favorite in the month. The function returns `null` when no favorite has a game in the month (which includes an empty `favoriteTeamIds` and an empty `monthDays`), when `teamIds` is `null` or `undefined` (a day BT holds no schedule for, or a month cached before S136), when `teamIds` is `[]` (no games: A8 dims that day already), or when every favorite with a game in the month plays. Otherwise it returns `{ mark: "red" | "yellow", off: number[] }`: `off` lists the favorites with a game in the month that are not in `teamIds`, in `favoriteTeamIds`' order, each once; `mark` is `red` when none of them plays and `yellow` when at least one plays but not all (A8: "at least one"). Unit tests, each with a `monthDays` in which every favorite plays at least once unless the case says otherwise: no favorites; `null`; `undefined`; `[]`; one favorite, off (red); two favorites, both off (red, `off` in the fan's order, not `teamIds`' or numeric order); two favorites, one plays (yellow, `off` the other); every favorite plays (`null`); a favorite listed twice in `favoriteTeamIds` (named once in `off`); an empty `monthDays` (`null` on every day); a `monthDays` whose days all have `teamIds` `null` (`null`); **favorites Rays (139) and Yankees (147) in a month before 1998** (`monthDays` lists 147 and never 139): a Yankees game day is `null`, and a day with games but no Yankees game is `red` with `off` `[147]`, so no day of that month is marked for the Rays or names them; **a Yankees-only fan in an October after they are eliminated** (`monthDays` holds only other teams' postseason games, never 147): every day is `null`; **a month the Yankees play** (a July, Yankees-only fan): a normal Yankees off day while other teams play is `red` with `off` `[147]`, and the All-Star Game's day (`teamIds` only the two league teams, 159 and 160) is `red` with `off` `[147]`, both still marked, while a day of the break with no games at all (`[]`) stays `null` as above.
