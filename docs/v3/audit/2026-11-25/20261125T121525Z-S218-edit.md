**Gap:** Three design capture specs pick scoreboard games by where they sit on the page rather than which game they are: `e2e/design/S20.spec.ts:75` (`getByTestId("scoreboard-module").nth(1)`), `e2e/design/S170.spec.ts:234-249` and `e2e/design/S74.spec.ts:138` (`.first()`, commented as SF @ NYM, the day's first game).
