**Gap:** The Plays room's Runs view lists every scoring play in one flat list, each row led by the batter's shortened name, with the inning tag, the pitcher and the score after the play (`web/src/components/pitch/PlaysPanel.tsx`; game-rooms `plays-scoring-*`). The owner said (2026-11-23): *"right now I can't tell at a glance which team is the one that scored I have to know the names of the players"*; *"So I need them separated by, like, it should show me the team that's batting at the time and as like a header."*; *"What I actually want is the outcome to be the featured thing."*; *"It says the thing that happened is the main it's like home run is the is the result and then under that it uses the English description provided in the MLB API to say all the stuff that happened in that play"*; *"I also think that the names being shortened on these plays should only happen if if there's not enough room"*; and *"it says like double as the title, and then Ryan McMahon doubles 12 on a sharp line drive to right fielder Dominic Canzone."* The inning view's rows have the same shape: a closed row reads `A. **Impact:** Before S203, a fan reading a game's scoring plays had to know which team each player is on to tell who scored, and every play in the Plays room led with a shortened player name and hid what happened until it was opened. S203 impact: scoring plays sit under headers like "Top 4 · Chicago Cubs", and every play, in an inning or in the scoring plays, is led by what happened ("Double") with MLB's full description of the play under it before it is opened, as the owner approved.
