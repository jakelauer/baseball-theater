**Gap:** On a phone or tablet, a fan who updates while on a game page gets S135's after-update pointer aimed at a menu button the game page doesn't have: it runs its ten seconds unseen and doesn't show again for that release. The owner was asked (2026-11-23): *"On a phone or tablet, if you press Update Now while on a game page, the "Update complete" pointer has nothing to point at: game pages have no menu button. It runs its 10 seconds unseen and won't show again for that release. What should it do?"* and answered *"Open the changelog in a modal on click"*. S192 designs it; nothing builds it.

**Impact:** Before S193, updating the app on a game page on a phone or tablet showed nothing a fan could see. S193 impact: they see that the update is complete and can open what changed in a window over the game, without losing their place, as the owner approved.

**Baseline:** On a game page at mobile and medium widths, where the game's top bar replaces the app header and no menu button shows, the after-update moment shows as shell-scoreboard A17's notice instead of S135's pointer (`UpdatedNotice`, `web/src/pwa/UpdatedNotice.tsx`): A9's popover with no arrow, its words, link and countdown line (`PointerBody`, `web/src/pwa/afterUpdate.tsx`), floating at the bottom right 16px (mobile) or 24px (medium) from the edges. A tap anywhere on it, its "Open the changelog" included, ends it and opens the change log over the game page (`ChangelogModal`): Mantine's `Modal` holding `ChangelogPage` itself (its ✕ in the title row through a new `onClose` prop), an inset card under the top bar on phones and a 688px dialog on tablets, over a 70% Dark scrim. It closes on its ✕, a tap outside, Escape or Back; opening it pushes a same-URL history entry that Back pops (the router's state kept), and closing it any other way pops it too. Every other page, and desktop, keeps S135's pointer. The notice and the pointer are one `useAfterUpdate` moment: moving between a game page and another page hands the rest of its 10 seconds over (`timeLeft`, the countdown line starting from it), the release is recorded once at startup (`checkAfterUpdate`), A6's box ends the notice but leaves an opened modal open. Design match pending.
