**Gap:** After S210, every player's name opens MLB.com. The owner asked (2026-11-23): *"We should have a user setting which is the user's preference for the default stats provider, and the default should be MLB.com because that is the most likely to definitely work. Patrons should have the ability to choose from the other providers, or the option to choose on click (so when clicking on a player they'd be presented with the options)"*. Settings live on the account and are for signed-in fans only (`AccountSettings`, `packages/domain/src/settings.ts`; FEATURES' Settings row: *"Hide settings for signed out users. Settings aren't available without signin"*), and what a patron may do comes from their pledge's tier (`entitlementsForTier`, `packages/domain/src/entitlements.ts`). S208 designs the setting and the picker; nothing builds them.

**Impact:** Before S211, a player's name always opened MLB.com. S211 impact: a signed-in fan has a Settings choice of stats site, MLB.com by default, and a patron can pick FanGraphs, Baseball-Reference or Baseball Savant instead, or have BT ask which site each time they click a name.
