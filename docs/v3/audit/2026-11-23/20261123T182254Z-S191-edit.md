**Gap:** S159's `favoriteTeamsOff` marks a favorite's off day on any day with a regular-season game anywhere (S167's per-day `regularSeason`) and counts any favorite with a game in the shown month, spring training included. So on a day with one regular-season game while everyone else plays spring training (the Seoul opener, 2024-03-20, or a Tokyo opener), a Yankees-only fan can see a red "Off day: Yankees" before the Yankees' season has begun, depending on whether they had a spring game that month; and at the season's other end, a one-day tiebreaker (2018-10-01) is a regular-season day for teams whose own seasons were already over. The owner was asked (2026-11-23): *"Should a favorite's off-day mark follow that team's own regular season, not just whether the day has any regular-season game? Today a day counts if any game that day is regular season. So on the Seoul or Tokyo opening day (one regular-season game while everyone else plays spring training), a Yankees-only fan can get a red "Off day: Yankees" before the Yankees' season has started, depending on whether they had a spring game. It's rare: a few days a year."* and chose *"Ship now, fix later (Recommended)"*, the option reading *"S159 ships as is with its comment corrected; a small follow-up story makes the mark follow each team's own season (it needs each game's type per team, which the month answer doesn't carry yet)."*

**Impact:** Before S191, a fan could be told their team had the day off on the few days a year when another team's season had started early or run late. S191 impact: a favorite's off day is marked only between that team's own first and last regular-season games.

**Source:** Feature: Calendar date picker

**Baseline:** **S191** landed: a favorite's off-day mark follows that team's own regular season. `ScheduleDayTally` and each day of `GET /api/v1/schedule/month/:month` (`ScheduleDayCount`) gain `regularSeasonTeamIds` (the teams of the day's `R` games, from `dayRegularSeasonTeamIds` in `packages/domain/src/schedule-month.ts`, which both schedule repository adapters call; `null` exactly when `regularSeason` is), and the month answer gains `inSeasonTeamIds` per day (`teamsInOwnSeason`: each team from its first to its last regular-season game of the calendar year, worked out from the year's stored days, which the month service now reads in one `listGameCounts` query, and one more for the filled days after an MLB fill; `null` where an unstored or untyped day leaves it unknown). A month inside the fill window now fills the year's missing days in its one MLB range request (owner, 2026-11-23: "Fetch the year"), so the mark works on this season's opening days, so `favoriteTeamsOff` counts only in-season favorites and falls back to S159's per-day rule when the field is `null` or missing.
