**Gap:** The Plays room is drawn by the ratified game-rooms design (S33; A6, A7): a boxed-inning line score, a floating inning sidebar pinned at the right, Runs as a flat list of scoring plays (*"inning tag, batter — result, pitcher and the score after the play"*), and an opened at-bat showing the pitcher and batter cards, the zone and trajectory, and its pitch list with the newest pitch open (`web/src/components/pitch/PlaysPanel.tsx`, `AtBatViewer.tsx`, `PitchTheater.tsx`, `PitchSequence.tsx`). The owner asked (2026-11-23) to rework all three parts:

- **Line score and innings:** *"In the plays panel the scoreboard thing with the innings on it is a little bit cramped somehow."*; *"I would also like it so that you could click on the inning, like, I want to be able to like click on the sixth inning as a whole column."*; *"I would want it to be on the side on desktop and it should be on the left, not the right. And And I want it to be on the bottom or the top on mobile."*
- **Scoring plays:** *"So I need them separated by, like, it should show me the team that's batting at the time and as like a header. So you end up with a lot of headers, but I think that's okay."*; *"What I actually want is the outcome to be the featured thing."*; *"it says like double as the title, and then Ryan McMahon doubles 12 on a sharp line drive to right fielder Dominic Canzone."*; *"I also think that the names being shortened on these plays should only happen if if there's not enough room"*.
- **Pitch detail:** *"I think that it should choose no pitches by default. It should just show you the list with none of them expanded. And if you expand one, it should dim the other pitches and make give you a better view of that one pitch by itself."*; *"Also, the side view and the top view should just be one thing that you can open up together."*; *"I think the list of pitches on desktop should be to the right side of the the like it should be the pitcher and batter boxes the strike zone and the top and side view if they're showing on the left and then the list of pitches on the right."*

No ratified frame draws any of it, so S201, S202 and S203 can't build it without designing in code (rule 18). The owner chose design stories for these reworks (2026-11-23, asked to send *"the bigger reworks (15, 21, 23, 24, 25, 30) through design stories"*: *"Your suggestion is fine"*).

**Impact:** Before S200, the Plays room's line score felt cramped, its inning picker floated over the plays, scoring plays didn't say which team scored, and every opened at-bat jumped straight to its last pitch. S200 impact: the owner approves a roomier line score whose innings can be clicked, a docked inning picker, scoring plays grouped under the batting team with what happened as the headline, and a pitch view that starts with no pitch chosen, before S201–S203 build them.
