**Gap:** The Plays room's Runs view lists every scoring play in one flat list, each row led by the batter's shortened name, with the inning tag, the pitcher and the score after the play (`web/src/components/pitch/PlaysPanel.tsx`; game-rooms `plays-scoring-*`). The owner said (2026-11-23): *"right now I can't tell at a glance which team is the one that scored I have to know the names of the players"*; *"So I need them separated by, like, it should show me the team that's batting at the time and as like a header."*; *"What I actually want is the outcome to be the featured thing."*; *"It says the thing that happened is the main it's like home run is the is the result and then under that it uses the English description provided in the MLB API to say all the stuff that happened in that play"*; *"I also think that the names being shortened on these plays should only happen if if there's not enough room"*. S200 designs it; nothing builds it.

**Impact:** Before S202, a fan reading a game's scoring plays had to know which team each player is on to tell who scored. S202 impact: scoring plays sit under headers like "Top 4 · Chicago Cubs", each led by what happened ("Double") with MLB's full description of the play under it, as the owner approved.
