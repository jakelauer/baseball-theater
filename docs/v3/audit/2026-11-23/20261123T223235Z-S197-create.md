**Gap:** The player lives inside the page that opened it (`PlayerHost` under the Videos room and Search), so leaving the page stops the video. The owner asked (2026-11-23) for *"a way to have the video playing like in the corner while you browse the site kind of like YouTube has, where you browse away from a video, but then it keeps playing the video in the corner, and you can go and open up and up again."*, more *"elegant and like automatic"* than the browser's picture-in-picture. S196 designs it; nothing builds it.

**Impact:** Before S197, opening the box score or the recap while watching a clip stopped the clip. S197 impact: the clip keeps playing in a corner while the fan looks around the site, and one tap opens it back up, as the owner approved.
