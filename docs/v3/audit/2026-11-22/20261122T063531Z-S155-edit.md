**Gap:** Before 1969 the American and National Leagues had no divisions, but BT's standings are built division by division: the mapper names each table from its teams' division (`functions/src/mappers/standings.ts:59-66`) and ranks teams by division rank (`:58`), and nothing in the repo shows what MLB answers for a day before 1969 (no committed fixture is older than 2024; the fixture client answers an empty table for a day it has no file for, `functions/src/adapters/fixtures/fixture-client.ts:157-181`). The owner chose one table per league for those days (owner, 2026-11-19, asked "What should Standings show for a date before 1969, when the leagues had no divisions?": chose *"One table per league (Recommended)"*; the option said AL and NL, each a single table, with the API work its own story before S138).

**Impact:** Before S155, the standings for a day before 1969 were untested and could come out empty, under a division's name, or out of order. S155 impact: a fan who looks up a day before 1969 sees two tables, the American League and the National League, each listing its teams in the order they stood that day.

**Source:** Feature: Standings
