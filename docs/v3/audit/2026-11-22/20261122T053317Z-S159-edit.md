**Acceptance criteria** (checks `scripts/verify-S159.sh` performs)

1. `favoriteTeamsOff(day, favoriteTeamIds, monthDays)` in `packages/domain/src/schedule-month.ts`, exported from `@bt/domain`, where `day` is the day asked about and `monthDays` the shown month's days, as S124 serves them with S136's `teamIds` and S167's `regularSeason`, each typed `{ teamIds?: readonly number[] | null; regularSeason?: boolean | null }` (so `ScheduleDayCount` fits, and so does a day cached before S136 or S167 that lacks either field). Two rules decide which favorites count:
    - **Only during the regular season** (owner, 2026-11-21, asked "Once a favorite team's season is over (eliminated, or no postseason), should later days in that same month stop being marked as its off days?": answered *"Only during the regular season"*): only a day whose `regularSeason` is `true` (it has a regular-season game) can carry a mark. A day whose `regularSeason` is `false` (only spring training, exhibition, All-Star or postseason games), `null` or missing (a day stored, or an answer cached, before S167) returns `null`: no red, no yellow, no tooltip.
    - **Only the favorites with games that month** (owner, 2026-11-21, asked "When a favorite team has no games at all in the month the calendar shows, should its days still be marked as off days?": chose *"Only teams with games that month (Recommended)"*: "a favorite that plays no game in the shown month is left out of the mark and the tooltip, so a team that didn't exist yet, or is out of the postseason, never marks a day."): a favorite whose ID is in no day's `teamIds` in `monthDays` is left out of the mark and of `off`, so a team that didn't exist yet marks no day of that month. A day whose `teamIds` is `null` or missing names no team, so it never puts a favorite in the month.

   The function returns `null` when the day carries no mark as above, when no favorite has a game in the month (which includes an empty `favoriteTeamIds` and an empty `monthDays`), when the day's `teamIds` is `null` or missing, when it is `[]` (no games: A8 dims that day already), or when every favorite with a game in the month plays. Otherwise it returns `{ mark: "red" | "yellow", off: number[] }`: `off` lists the favorites with a game in the month that are not in the day's `teamIds`, in `favoriteTeamIds`' order, each once; `mark` is `red` when none of them plays and `yellow` when at least one plays but not all (A8: "at least one"). Unit tests, each on a regular-season day (`regularSeason: true`) with a `monthDays` in which every favorite plays at least once unless the case says otherwise: no favorites; `teamIds` `null`; `teamIds` missing; `[]`; one favorite, off (red); two favorites, both off (red, `off` in the fan's order, not `teamIds`' or numeric order); two favorites, one plays (yellow, `off` the other); every favorite plays (`null`); a favorite listed twice in `favoriteTeamIds` (named once in `off`); an empty `monthDays` (`null`); a `monthDays` whose days all have `teamIds` `null` (`null`); `regularSeason` `false`, `null` and missing on a day a favorite is off (each `null`); **a Yankees-only fan in October 2023** (the Yankees' last game was October 1, the regular season's last day; the later days hold only other teams' postseason games, `regularSeason: false`): October 1 is `null` because they played, and a postseason day with other teams' games is `null`; **a regular-season day the Yankees don't play** while other teams do (a Yankees-only fan): `red` with `off` `[147]`; **favorites Rays (139) and Yankees (147) in a month before 1998** (`monthDays` lists 147 and never 139): a Yankees game day is `null`, and a regular-season day with games but no Yankees game is `red` with `off` `[147]`, so no day of that month is marked for the Rays or names them; **a July, Yankees-only fan**: a normal Yankees off day while other teams play is `red` with `off` `[147]`, the All-Star Game's day (`teamIds` only the two league teams, 159 and 160, `regularSeason: false`) is `null`, never `red`, so it carries no mark (owner, 2026-11-22, asked "Should the calendar mark the All-Star Game's day as an off day for favorite teams not playing in it?": chose *"No mark on the All-Star day (Recommended)"*; the option read: *"keep your "Only during the regular season" answer literal; the All-Star day looks like spring training and postseason-only days.
