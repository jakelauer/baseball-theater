**Gap:** BT knows each player only by MLB's id (MLBAM, e.g. `matchup.batter.id`, the box score's person ids). That id opens the player's page on MLB.com and on Baseball Savant, which MLB runs and keys by the same id, but FanGraphs and Baseball-Reference key their player pages by ids of their own, and BT has no way from one to the other: the recorded fixtures carry no cross-reference (`fixtures/raw/people-823823.json` has none). The owner asked (2026-11-23) that *"Patrons should have the ability to choose from the other providers"* when a player's name opens their stats, so BT needs those ids. And a player's name in BT opens nothing: the pitcher and batter cards of an opened play (`web/src/components/pitch/MatchupCards.tsx`, shared with the Live room) and the box score's tables (`web/src/components/game/BoxRoom.tsx`) are plain text. The owner said (2026-11-23): *"I should be able to click on their names to open their stats, just like in the box score. Well, I guess the box score needs that."*; and, asked how: *"We should have a user setting which is the user's preference for the default stats provider, and the default should be MLB.com because that is the most likely to definitely work. Patrons should have the ability to choose from the other providers, or the option to choose on click (so when clicking on a player they'd be presented with the options)"*. Settings live on the account and are for signed-in fans only (`AccountSettings`, `packages/domain/src/settings.ts`; FEATURES' Settings row: *"Hide settings for signed out users. Settings aren't available without signin"*), and what a patron may do comes from their pledge's tier (`entitlementsForTier`, `packages/domain/src/entitlements.ts`). On 2026-11-25 the owner dropped the design story (S208), and with it the choose-on-click option: *"I think S195 and S199 could be built without designs, using existing paradigms as a guide. I don't want to require a complete 1:1 match between designs and the site at all times. Sometimes it's easier to iterate on the site itself. S197 has more to do with behavior than designs - it's just a floating video player in the corner and should use exactly the same behaviors that YouTube's mini player uses. S208 doesn't need a design either - the only parts that could are the setting and the option to pick every time. We could ditch that pick every time part, and the setting is pretty self-evident."*; and folded S210 and S211 into this story: *"Also S209-211 should just be one story"*.

**Impact:** Before S209, a fan who wanted a player's stats had to leave BT and search for the player. S209 impact: a player's name on a play or in the box score opens that player's stats page, on MLB.com by default; a signed-in fan has a Settings choice of stats site, and a patron can pick FanGraphs, Baseball-Reference or Baseball Savant instead.

**Source:** Feature: Multi-source knowledge; Settings (favorites, hide scores, playback prefs)

**Baseline:** A player's name on an opened play's pitcher and batter cards (`MatchupCards`, shared with the Live room) and in each box score table (batting and pitching) is one link component, `PlayerLink` (`web/src/components/PlayerLink.tsx`), opening in a new tab the page `playerProfileUrl(effectiveStatsProvider(settings, entitlements), ids)` builds (`packages/domain/src/player-links.ts`, beside `StatsProvider`): MLB.com for a signed-out visitor and by default, and for a patron (`Entitlements.statsProviderChoice`, Backer and up, derived from the tier by `entitlementsForTier` and never stored) the site saved as `AccountSettings.statsProvider` (column `stats_provider`, default `'mlb'`), chosen on Settings' Stats Site control, whose three other sites are disabled and marked "Patron option" without the entitlement; the settings route refuses a change to a site other than MLB.com without it, and a refused save puts the account's saved copy back. The FanGraphs and Baseball-Reference ids are kept in `player_ids` behind `PlayerIdRepository`, about 24,000 rows: each row of the Chadwick Bureau's open register (ODC-By 1.0; `ChadwickPlayerRegister` behind the `PlayerRegister` port) with an MLB id and either id, an id the register lacks for a player it says played in MLB filled from MLB's `xrefId` cross-references (`fangraphs`, and `lahman` for Baseball-Reference; `PlayerXrefSource`, implemented by `HttpMlbStatsClient`), the register's id kept wherever both hold one.
