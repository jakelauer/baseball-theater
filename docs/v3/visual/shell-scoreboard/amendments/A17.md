**Adds:** on a game page at mobile and medium widths, in place of A9's pointer, the after-update notice: A9's popover with no arrow, floating at the bottom right, which the fan taps to open the change log in a modal over the game page; that modal, holding A9's change log page (its title and its releases, loading, empty and error states), as a card inset 16px from the screen's edges under the game's top bar on mobile and a dialog on medium; how it closes.

## Rules

- **Where it applies:** on a game page (`/game/:pk`, any room) at the mobile and medium pairings, the layouts where the game's top bar replaces the app header and no menu button shows (owner, 2026-11-23, chose the reading *"On game pages (phone/tablet) the notice is tappable and opens the changelog in a modal over the game; elsewhere the pointer stays as built."*). Every other page, desktop included, keeps A9's pointer as built (S135): aimed at the menu button on mobile and medium, at the rail's `Changelog` link on desktop, game pages on desktop included, since the rail stays there.
- **The notice:** shows when A9's pointer would (a load whose release is newer than the one recorded), in A9's words, with no arrow, at the bottom right. The fan taps it, anywhere on it, to open the change log in the modal; the notice goes as the modal opens. Its link `Open the changelog` does the same as the rest of it: it opens the modal, not the `/changelog` page, so the fan stays on the game.
- **The modal:** the change log opens over the game page, which stays where it was under the scrim (its live updates keep arriving behind it), as a card inset 16px from the screen's edges on mobile (under the game's top bar) and a dialog on medium. It shows A9's change log page inside, title included, in A9's states: the releases, loading, empty (no approved release notes) and error with Retry, the same request and the same words as the `/changelog` page. The URL doesn't change. Hide Scores changes nothing in it (release notes carry no scores, A9).
- **How it closes:** its ✕; a tap on the dimmed game page outside the card or dialog; Escape; and a phone's Back button or gesture, which closes the modal and stays on the game page instead of leaving it. Closing it returns the fan to the game page as they left it, and it doesn't open again on that load.
- **A9's other rules:** all hold as A9 has them. Never with the update notice: whenever A6's box is up, the notice doesn't show and a notice that was showing goes at once and doesn't come back on that load, as A9's pointer does (the modal, once the fan has opened it, stays open). Ten seconds: the notice goes after 10 seconds, its countdown line running them down, never pausing (A9's Q13); a tap opens the modal instead of only dismissing it, so the owner's *"dismiss on click"* becomes *"Open the changelog in a modal on click"* on these pages; the modal itself has no timer. Once per release: it shows only on the first load of a newer release, as A9's pointer, recorded the same way (S135 AC 3). Reduced motion: the notice neither fades nor slides and draws no countdown line, as A9's pointer, and the modal opens and closes without sliding.
- **Moving between pages:** the notice and A9's pointer are one after-update moment on one load. If the fan leaves the game page while the notice shows (its back arrow to the scoreboard, say), the rest of its 10 seconds run as A9's pointer there, aimed at the menu button; if the fan opens a game page while A9's pointer shows, the rest run as this notice. Turning a tablet so the layout becomes desktop switches the same way.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them.

- What happens on a game page (owner, 2026-11-23, asked *"On a phone or tablet, if you press Update Now while on a game page, the "Update complete" pointer has nothing to point at: game pages have no menu button. It runs its 10 seconds unseen and won't show again for that release. What should it do?"*): "Open the changelog in a modal on click"
- How to read that (owner, 2026-11-23, asked *"On a phone or tablet, after you update while on a game page, should the "Update complete" notice be something you tap that opens the changelog in a window over the game page, without leaving it? That's the reading of your "Open the changelog in a modal on click" answer; S192 (its design) needs it confirmed before drawing."*): "Yes, that reading", the option reading *"On game pages (phone/tablet) the notice is tappable and opens the changelog in a modal over the game; elsewhere the pointer stays as built."*
- The phone's modal (owner, 2026-11-23, asked *"Do you ratify shell-scoreboard A17, the "Update complete" notice on game pages (phone and tablet) that opens the changelog in a window over the game?"*, chose "Ratify with changes", then asked which changes): "Add a margin around the modal on mobile", and again later: "S192: add a margin around the mobile model … Modal". Not a ratification: A17 was redrawn with the margin and went back to the owner.
- Ratification (owner, 2026-11-23, asked *"Do you ratify shell-scoreboard A17, the "Update complete" notice on game pages (phone and tablet) that opens the changelog in a window over the game, now with a 16px margin around the window on phones (rounded corners, outline, soft shadow; images sent earlier)?"*): chose "Ratify as drawn".

## Choices made while drawing (accepted at ratification)

- *Agent:* the notice keeps A9's words, link line and countdown, drops only the arrow, and sits at the bottom right, where A6's update notice was when the fan pressed Update Now (16px from the edges on mobile, 24px on medium).
- *Agent:* the link `Open the changelog` opens the modal too, so nothing on the notice leaves the game page.
- *Agent:* on mobile the modal is a card under the game's top bar, keeping the top bar visible under the scrim so the fan sees they are still on the game; on medium it is a centered 688px dialog. Both use A8's 70% Dark scrim and ✕.
- *Agent:* the phone's margin (the owner's change, ## Decisions) is 16px on every side, the shell's phone gutter and the notice's own distance from the edges, and the card takes the medium dialog's 12px corners, full hairline edge and shadow, since it no longer meets the screen's edges.
- *Agent:* Back closes the modal and stays on the game page (A8's calendar, by contrast, leaves Back alone: the owner's *"calendar being open does not affect back button"*).
- *Agent:* if the fan moves between a game page and another page while the after-update moment is up, the notice and A9's pointer hand over to each other for the rest of its 10 seconds.
