# A13 — A Live marker replaces "Fetched … ago" (S147)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A12](A12.md), and through them the footers of the ratified game-shell, game-rooms and standings-search-settings frames. Story **S147**; built by S148 (the scoreboard's own live connection is S149). Ratified 2026-11-19.

**Adds:** the Live marker (component `live-marker`): an 8px red dot (`Primary Light`, #FF5247) with a red glow, and the words "Live Data · Active" beside it (11px IBM Plex Sans Medium, primary text), centred in a full-width strip at the top of the footer of any page that has an open live WebSocket (`footer-live-strip`: 10px above and below, a fill slightly darker than the footer, black at 40% over `Dark`, about #0A0A09). The dot glows and blinks slowly: it fades between the `Phase=Bright` and `Phase=Dim` variants and back, 2 seconds a cycle, easing in and out. With reduced motion it stays at `Phase=Bright`, glowing, and does not blink.
**Overrides:** the footer line "Fetched {age} · baseball.theater" (the `footer-meta` component, and every ratified frame that draws it: the shell-scoreboard frames, game-shell A3, game-rooms A4, shell-scoreboard A4, the Standings frames). The whole line goes, "Fetched {age}" and "baseball.theater" both, on every page (owner, 2026-11-19: "baseball.theater is supposed to be removed"). On a page with an open live WebSocket the footer opens with the strip, "● Live Data · Active" centred in it; on every other page there is no strip. Also overrides the offline footer line "Offline · saved {age}" (A1, game-rooms A1): it goes too, since the offline banner already says it (owner, 2026-11-19: "yes"). Those frames stay sealed and differ from a build only in this line.
**Repeals:** the "Fetched {age}" label, and ADR-014's "Freshness is visible" row as far as it asked for that label (ARCHITECTURE.md, amended in the same change set as the story).

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-11-10: "just get rid of the fetched label" (S141). Asked on 2026-11-16 whether ADR-014's "freshness is visible" promise (and VISUAL-DESIGN D9) should be dropped now that the "Fetched … ago" line is going: "Remove the fetch label and replace it with a red dot and Live at the bottom of any page that has an active websocket, with the red dot glowing and slowly blinking". Told the scoreboard only re-fetched (2026-11-16): "the scoreboard should have a live connection"; asked when it connects: "While a game is live"; asked how: "One socket per day". Shown the draft on 2026-11-19: "the footer should have the Live indicator at the top, centered, with a slightly darker bg color than the footer, and it shouldn't have “baseball. Theater” next to it. Change it to say “Live Data - Active” (with a middle dot instead of hyphen)". Seeing the redrawn frames and the site footer's (A14), the same day: "S147 didn't yet remove the baseball theater link, and the live data bar still shows under the footer". Asked, about A14, "Offline: drop the footer's 'Offline · saved …' line, since the offline banner already says it? This answer also settles the same point in A13", the same day: "yes". Asked "Do you ratify A13, the Live marker as it's drawn in Figma now? Every live page's footer opens with a full-width, slightly darker strip reading '● Live Data · Active', centred, with 'baseball.theater' alone below it. Pages without a live connection have no strip.", the same day: "baseball.theater is supposed to be removed, which I've asked for twice. Once that's gone, it's ratified." The `site-footer` component's "baseball.theater" line was then removed from all four of its variants, so no frame draws it.

What the amendment settles (agent's reading of those words, ratified with the frames):

- **Marker:** an 8px dot in `Primary Light` (#FF5247, VISUAL-DESIGN's readable red on dark) with a drop-shadow glow of the same red, and the words "Live Data · Active" (a middle dot, not a hyphen; owner, 2026-11-19) in 11px IBM Plex Sans Medium, primary text, 6px from the dot. The words are what a screen reader announces; the dot is decoration.
- **Strip:** the marker sits alone, centred, in `footer-live-strip`, a full-width band at the very top of the footer, 10px above and below the marker, filled slightly darker than the footer (black at 40% over `Dark`, about #0A0A09; no token is darker than `Dark`). Nothing sits beside the marker, and no footer has a "baseball.theater" line (owner, 2026-11-19: "at the top, centered, with a slightly darker bg color than the footer"; then, seeing it still there: "S147 didn't yet remove the baseball theater link, and the live data bar still shows under the footer"; and "baseball.theater is supposed to be removed, which I've asked for twice"). Reused: the `footer-meta` line's type and centring, and the shape of the game top bar's `live-indicator` (a dot and a word); its green (`Accent 2`) is not reused, since the owner asked for red.
- **One component everywhere:** the strip lives in one Figma component set, `site-footer` (Components page; its `Live=On` variants), and every frame's footer is an instance of it, so the strip appears in every frame that draws a page with an open live WebSocket and a change to it reaches them all (owner, 2026-11-19: "The footer changes (both of them) aren't updated in all the designs. The footer should be based on modules referenced everywhere so they all update automatically (which is generally how all shared design components should work)"). S141's A14 owns the rest of that footer.
- **Blink:** the dot fades from `Phase=Bright` (opaque, glow 8px) to `Phase=Dim` (45%, glow 4px) and back, 2 seconds a full cycle, easing in and out, for as long as the socket is open. The words do not blink.
- **Reduced motion:** with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the dot stays at `Phase=Bright`, glowing, and does not blink (VISUAL-DESIGN already stops the live pulse under reduced motion).
- **Which pages:** a page shows the marker exactly while it holds an open live WebSocket. Two pages open one: a game page whose game is in its live window (2 hours before first pitch to 3 hours after the game), and the scoreboard while any game on its day is in its live window (one socket per day, S149). Standings, Search and Settings open none. A page that later opens one shows the marker with no further design.
- **No socket:** every other page's footer has no strip and no "baseball.theater" line; no page shows "Fetched … ago" or any age. Until S142 builds the footer of links, such a page ends with no footer line at all.
- **Reconnecting:** while the socket reconnects after a drop, the marker and its strip are hidden, as on a page with no socket; it comes back, blinking, once the socket is open again. So it never claims pushes that aren't arriving.
- **Polling:** once a page has given up reconnecting and fallen back to polling (`LIVE_TIMING.maxFailures`), the marker and its strip stay hidden; polling is not live.
- **Site footer:** when S141's footer of links replaces this line (built by S142), the strip keeps its place at the very top of that footer, above the link columns, centred, with nothing beside it; S141's amendment draws it there, and A13's frames already show it so, through the shared `site-footer` component.
- **Offline:** the offline footer line ("Offline · saved … ago", A1 and game-rooms A1) goes as well, since the offline banner already says the page is offline and how old its scores are (owner, 2026-11-19, asked about A14 and told the answer settles A13 too: "yes"); offline there is no socket, so no marker.
- **Placement:** "at the bottom" is the footer, at the end of the page's content, not pinned to the bottom of the screen; the strip is the footer's first row.

## Open questions

1. **Q1.** **Is it right that the scoreboard shows no Live marker, since it refreshes by polling every 15 seconds rather than over a WebSocket?** As first drawn, the scoreboard had no marker, to your words "any page that has an active websocket". The other reading: the scoreboard shows it too while any game on it is live. **Answered 2026-11-16:** *"the scoreboard should have a live connection"*, opened only *"While a game is live"*, through *"One socket per day"* (S149 builds it). Redrawn 2026-11-18: `live-marker-scoreboard-live-*` show the scoreboard with a game in its live window ending in the marker, and `live-marker-scoreboard-quiet-*` show a day with no game in its window and no marker.
2. **Q2.** **Should the footer's red "● Live" mean "this page is receiving updates", shown whenever the page's live connection is open (including before first pitch and after the last out), and is it clear enough beside the top bar's green "● LIVE", or should it show only while the game is being played, or use another word?** On a live game page the top bar already shows a green "● LIVE" (`Accent 2`) meaning the game is in progress; the footer's red "● Live" means this page is receiving updates as they happen. Both are drawn in `live-marker-game-mobile` and `live-marker-game-desktop` (Figma, Shell & navigation page, under the "AB" label). A game page's connection is open from 2 hours before first pitch to 3 hours after the game, so as drafted a game page an hour before first pitch (top bar reads Scheduled), or 2 hours after the last out (top bar reads Final), also ends in "● Live · baseball.theater" while its top bar has no "● LIVE". The answers lead to: (a) keep it as drafted: the marker shows whenever the connection is open and reads "Live" everywhere, and the frames stand; (b) keep the meaning but change the word (for example "Live updates"): every frame's footer is redrawn with that word before ratifying; (c) show it only while the game is in progress: the marker follows the game's state, not just the connection, so a Scheduled or Final game page (and the scoreboard with no game in progress) reads "baseball.theater" alone, and the Which pages decision is rewritten to say so before ratifying. **Answered 2026-11-19:** *"the footer should have the Live indicator at the top, centered, with a slightly darker bg color than the footer, and it shouldn't have “baseball. Theater” next to it. Change it to say “Live Data - Active” (with a middle dot instead of hyphen)"*. Read as answer (b): the meaning stays (the marker shows whenever the page's live connection is open, before first pitch and after the last out included), and the words "Live Data · Active" say it is the page's data connection, not the game, that is live, so it no longer reads as a second "LIVE" beside the top bar's. Redrawn 2026-11-19: every live frame's footer opens with the darker strip, the marker centred in it, and "baseball.theater" alone below it; that line was then removed from every frame at the owner's word (Decisions).
