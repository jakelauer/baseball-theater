# A18 — Short page: the content area fills the window, so the footer never sits high (B525)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its ratified amendment [A14](A14.md) (the site footer, ratified 2026-11-19), whose **Short page** decision put the footer right after the content with the screen's leftover height empty below it. Bug **B525** (issue #525); the fix its group built is what this draws. Ratified 2026-11-24 (## Ratification). No story: the owner ratified it in session while B525 was being fixed, as with the other owner-directed amendments.

**Adds:** a minimum height for the page shell: at least the window's height, the content area taking whatever height the header and the footer leave, so on a short page the footer ends at the window's bottom edge.
**Overrides:** A14's **Short page** decision, "on a page shorter than the screen the leftover height is empty below it", and the two A14 frames that draw that empty space under the footer: `site-footer-short-desktop` (redrawn here as `site-footer-short-fill-desktop`) and `site-footer-medium`'s "with the screen's leftover height empty below it" (not redrawn; this text governs where its footer sits), and any other sealed frame that draws blank space below its footer. A14's files stay sealed in its manifest.
**Repeals:** nothing. A14's "It is never pinned over content or to the screen's bottom edge" stands.

## Rule

- **Never pinned:** the footer has no sticky or fixed position. It is part of the page's flow and scrolls with it, as A14 says.
- **The shell fills the window:** the page shell is at least the window's height (`min-height: 100dvh`, with `100vh` as the fallback where `dvh` isn't supported), and the content area between the header and the footer takes the remaining space.
- **Short page:** when the content is shorter than the window, the content area grows, so the footer ends at the window's bottom edge and the blank space is above it, under the content, never below the footer.
- **Tall page:** when the content is taller than the window, nothing changes: the footer follows the content and is reached by scrolling, as before.
- **Everywhere the footer shows:** this holds on every page A14's **Where it shows** names, at every pairing; the installed app keeps A1's home-indicator safe area below the footer (A14's **Installed app**). The content inside the content area keeps its own alignment (top-aligned on the scoreboard); only the area's height changes.

## Components

The `site-footer` component set (705:23532) does not change, so no footer instance anywhere changes. The change is the page's layout, not a shared piece: in the redrawn frame the content column's "Games section" fills the height and its "Footer region" hugs the footer, where A14's frame had the region fill the leftover height with the footer at its top. No other frame is redrawn: wherever a sealed frame, on any surface, draws blank space below its footer, this rule governs where that footer sits, as for the two named under **Overrides**.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them.

- The bug (owner, B525, 2026-11-24): "The content area has no minimum height, so the footer can appear in the middle of the page. Ideally, the footer behaves as sticky to the bottom of the window unless the content pushes it past that."
- Whether B525 overrides A14's "never pinned" rule (owner, 2026-11-24): "It's never pinned, but the content's minimum height should be either a percentage of the viewport height or something similar to avoid a highwater footer. I phrased it the way I did because I didn't know if there was a more elegant CSS option than expanding the content container arbitrarily"
- Ratification (owner, 2026-11-24, asked *"Ratify an amendment to the shell design's footer rule (A14, "Short page"): the footer is still never pinned, but on a short page the content area fills at least the window's height, so the footer ends at the window's bottom edge instead of sitting high with blank space under it … This updates A14's text and its short-page frame (site-footer-short-desktop) to match what group E built."*): chose "Ratify the A14 amendment".

## Choices made while drawing (accepted at ratification)

- *Agent:* the minimum height is the whole shell's, at the window's height (`100dvh`, `100vh` fallback), with the content area flexing to fill it, rather than a fixed percentage on the content area alone, so the footer lands exactly at the window's bottom edge whatever the header's and footer's heights; this is the "something similar" the owner allowed, and what the ratified question described.
- *Agent:* the redrawn frame is a new frame under its own name, leaving A14's frame and sealed PNG as they were; `site-footer-medium` is governed by this text rather than redrawn, since its only difference is where the footer sits.
