# A8 — The scoreboard's calendar date picker (S123)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A7](A7.md). Story **S123**; built by S125 (counts from S124, favorite teams from S136). **Draft, not ratified.** Redrawn 2026-11-16 to the owner's review of 2026-11-09 and answers of 2026-11-10 (see Decisions).

**Adds:** a calendar that opens from the scoreboard's date: a date field the user can type into (and its invalid states: not a date, and a date before 1901-04-18), native month and year pickers with ‹ › month arrows, a month grid with its weekday row, a subtle border and six week rows, each day's game count (`6 gm`), days with no games dimmed but pickable, a mark in the top right corner of each day one of the fan's favorite teams plays, a month still loading its counts, a month whose counts failed (no message), an off-season month, the earliest bound (nothing before 1901-04-18 can be picked), and a short-screen phone sheet with the counts dropped. On mobile it opens as a bottom sheet; on medium and desktop as a dropdown under the date.
**Overrides:** the date control's label in `scoreboard-mobile`, `-medium`, `-desktop` (and in every other ratified frame that draws it, ‹ `SEP 21, 2026` ›) becomes a button: a calendar glyph, the date, and a `▾`, inside a hairline outline. The previous / next-day arrows stay.
**Repeals:** nothing.

## Behaviour

What the calendar does that a still frame can't show, from AC 1 and the owner's answers (Decisions).

- **Container.** Mobile: always the bottom sheet, never a full-screen dialog, with no drag handle and no dragging. Medium and desktop: the dropdown under the date button.
- **Speed.** The calendar opens already filled: the month it opens on is the month of the day the scoreboard shows, whose counts S125 loads with the scoreboard, so its first frame has every count, with no loading state, no fade and no reflow. The sheet slides up in 200ms (ease-out) with its content already in it; the dropdown appears at once, with no transition. The loading state (the `loading` frames) is only ever drawn for a month the fan moves to.
- **Closing.** No `Close` button. Mobile has the ✕; medium and desktop have none. A click or tap outside the calendar closes it at every size, and so does Esc, which closes it without changing the date. On mobile, opening the calendar and closing it without choosing a day (the ✕, a tap outside, Esc) never changes the URL or adds to the browser's history. Choosing a day, or `Today` on today's month, navigates to that day as the one-day arrows do (`/games/<date>`), and the calendar closes.
- **Back.** Because the open calendar is not in the URL, a phone's Back button or gesture while the sheet is open does what it does with the calendar closed (it goes back to the previous page, say from `/games/2026-09-21` to `/games/2026-09-20`), and the sheet closes with it: S125 closes the calendar whenever the location changes, since the scoreboard stays mounted across days.
- **Today.** `Today` stays. While the calendar shows today's month it picks today and closes, the same as tapping today's day. While it shows any other month it moves the calendar to today's month and leaves it open. "Today" is the day the scoreboard opens on, the server's answer from `useScoreboardDefaultDate` (`web/src/api/scoreboardDate.ts`, which falls back to `mlbToday` only when the server can't answer), never the device's own clock; when the season is over that is the last season's final day. While that day is still loading, `Today` is drawn in muted text and does nothing.
- **Month arrows.** ‹ and › in the calendar's header move it one month; on a January ‹ and on a December › cross into the neighbouring year and the year picker follows. At April 1901 ‹ is disabled (20%), as the `earliest` frames draw it. At December of next year › is disabled the same way. The one-day ‹ › beside the date control stay.
- **Month and year pickers.** Native selects. The year picker lists 1901 through next year. In 1901 the month picker's January, February and March are disabled; a choice that lands before April 1901 (choosing 1901 while the month is January to March) shows April 1901 instead. Neither picker changes the date; they only change the month shown.
- **A month after December of next year.** Only a typed date reaches one (future dates are pickable). The date navigates and the calendar shows that month with each day's bold number, no counts, nothing dimmed, nothing marked and no message (S124 answers 400 `month_too_late`, read as "no counts", not an error); the year picker reads that year, added as its last option while it is shown; › is disabled and ‹ works.
- **An unknown month.** When S136's month function answers unknown (no day with games and at least one day BT holds no schedule for, such as December 2019, where every count is `null` because S122's backfill skips it), the month is drawn like the `failed` frames: bold numbers, no counts, nothing dimmed, nothing marked and no message, so it never looks like an off-season month. A `null` day in a month that has games is drawn like a no-game day: dimmed, no count, pickable.
- **No-game and disabled days.** Dimming (35%, pickable) applies only on or after 1901-04-18; a day before it gets only the disabled style (20%, no count, not focusable), so the two never stack.
- **Favorite-team mark.** Drawn only when the fan has more than 0 favorite teams, on each day at least one of them plays, from the teams S136 returns for each day. A signed-out fan, or one with no favorites, sees no marks. A month still loading or whose counts failed shows none.
- **Height.** Every month reserves six week rows, so the calendar never changes height between months (an error line under the date field adds one line while it shows). On a phone whose screen is under 640px tall (the window's inner height when the calendar opens) the game counts are dropped and the cells are 36px tall, as the `short` frame draws; that is decided once from the screen, never per month. Under 500px, where even that sheet doesn't fit under the top bar, the grid scrolls inside the sheet.

## Components

What S125 builds from Mantine's own date components (`@mantine/dates`, Mantine 7; S125 adds the package beside `@mantine/core` and its `dayjs` peer), which parts are native controls and which are custom, so nothing is designed in code. Behaviour named here was checked against `@mantine/dates` 7.17.8's source.

- **Mantine `Month`** (`@mantine/dates`): the month grid of days and its weekday row, used on its own (not inside `DatePicker`, whose header draws its own title button; this header is custom). `firstDayOfWeek={0}` gives the `SU`-first weekday row; `weekdayFormat="dd"` uppercased by style gives `SU MO …`; `withCellSpacing={false}` lets the days meet so the grid's hairlines can be drawn between them; `hideOutsideDates` leaves the cells before the 1st and after the last day empty (S124 answers one month per request, so a neighbouring month's days would have no count and would look like days with no games). `minDate={new Date(1901, 3, 18)}` and no `maxDate`: `Month` marks every day before 1901-04-18 `disabled`, styled as the earliest frames draw them. `getDayProps` passes `selected` for the date's day and the dimmed style (35% opacity, without `disabled`, so it stays pickable) for a day with no games on or after 1901-04-18.
- **`renderDay`** on that `Month`: draws each day as its bold number over its game count (`6 gm`, 11px, 50% opacity), or the loading bar while the month's counts load, plus the favorite-team mark in the top right corner on a day one of the fan's favorite teams plays.
- **`DatesProvider`** with `settings={{ consistentWeeks: true }}`: every month gets six week rows (`getMonthDays` pads a shorter month with the next weeks). Mantine hides an outside day with `display: none`, so S125 gives the day cell a fixed height through the Styles API, or a padded sixth row would collapse.
- **Mantine `DateInput`**: the date field the user types into (`valueFormat="MMM D, YYYY"`), with its `error` prop drawing the invalid states (Primary Light outline and message). Its own drop-down calendar is turned off with `popoverProps={{ disabled: true }}`, so the one calendar on screen is the `Month` below it, kept in step with the field. How S125 wires it, because DateInput's defaults fight the drawn states:
  - One strict `dateParser` reads typed forms such as `Sep 21, 2026`, `9/21/2026` and `2026-09-21`. A typed date counts only if it formats back to the same year, month and day, so `Feb 30, 2026` is "Not a date.", not March 2.
  - S125 does not navigate from DateInput's `onChange`, which fires on every keystroke that parses (`9/2` on the way to `9/21` is September 2). On Enter, or when the field loses focus, it parses the field's text with that parser and either navigates or sets `error`.
  - `fixOnBlur={false}`, so leaving the field keeps the typed text and its error instead of quietly restoring the last valid date.
  - No `minDate` on the `DateInput`: with one, Mantine silently ignores a parsed date before the bound and shows no error. The parser checks the bound and shows "No data before Apr 18, 1901." (the `tooearly` frames).
- **Native month and year pickers**: two native `<select>` elements (Mantine core's `NativeSelect`), one for the month and one for the year, so tapping either opens the device's own picker. Styled as the outlined controls drawn in the header (`color/bg/page` fill, hairline outline, a `▾`).
- **Mantine `Popover`** (medium, desktop; `transitionProps={{ duration: 0 }}`) and **`Drawer`** with `position="bottom"` (mobile; `withCloseButton={false}`, since the ✕ is drawn in the label row; a 200ms slide-up transition; no handle). Both close on Esc and on a click outside, which is Mantine's default.
- **Custom:** the date button (calendar glyph, date, `▾`, hairline outline, Raised current while open) that replaces the plain label in `DateControl.tsx`; the header row (‹, the two native pickers, ›) and its ends (disabled at April 1901 and December of next year); the ✕; the grid's border and hairlines; the per-day game count text, its loading bar and the favorite-team mark; the "No games this month." footer line; the `Today` footer and its two behaviours; closing on a location change; the short-screen rule; and the theme styles mapping the selected day to Primary, disabled days to 20%, and the panel to `color/bg/raised`.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them.

- The calendar (owner, 2026-11-07): "I also want a calendar picker UI that makes it easy to switch the year, lets you pick with the calendar visible and the day of the week showing in the calendar grid, and the date display should let you type the date in yourself. If we could also show the number of games on each day in that view that would be great."
- No-game days (owner, 2026-11-07, asked whether they are dimmed): "dimmed". They stay pickable.
- Future dates (owner, 2026-11-07, asked whether they are allowed): "allow future".
- The owner's review of the first draft (2026-11-09): "No need to show a message about failure to load game counts"; "Remove the instructional label about typing or picking"; "Change the message about 1901 to say that there’s no data before that first date. Keep it as simple as that."; "Get rid of the “games start” label"; "Add a very subtle border for the grid of days".
- The favorite-team mark (owner, 2026-11-09): "If the user has >0 favorite teams, make the top right corner of the days at least one of their teams is playing. This means the API for that needs to return the teams that played each day."
- Switching month and year (owner, 2026-11-09, asked "How do you trigger the years grid? Can it use a native OS UI to pick a year?" and then offered native month and year dropdowns, a native year only, or drawn grids of months and years): "Native month + year (Recommended)". So no year or decade view is drawn.
- Q1, sheet on mobile and a dropdown on medium and desktop (owner, 2026-11-10): "yes, but it should be super speedy. Like, I don't want to be waiting for the calendar to load the month games thing. It should already be loaded. Like, the schedule should already be loaded enough to show that the current month. So it should be super speedy, not a slow animation or anything like that. It should have the content in it when it slides up. I don't want to see it like reflowing or like fade in. I want it to be very smooth, and it should feel app-like, just speedy and smooth." On the sheet's handle: "we don't even need it to be draggable, because you can't drag it up, and dragging it down doesn't do anything except dismiss it." On short screens: "So, actually, yeah, scratch that. I want it to always be the drawer, but if it's too short, we should hide the game counts or something like that. I don't know. I'd like to hear your thoughts on that one." Shown the approach drawn here (six week rows always, counts dropped by screen height, decided once, the grid scrolling only as a last resort, a ✕ on phones only, no handle): "Number two, yes."
- Q2, the bare number under each day (owner, 2026-11-09): "Add “gm” to the game counts (eg “6 gm”)". And (owner, 2026-11-10): "The smaller numbers underneath the calendar dates should be 50% opaque and the date numbers should be bolded".
- Q3, the `Today` and `Close` buttons (owner, 2026-11-10): "the close button is unnecessary, except on mobile, we might need that. Clicking outside of it should always trigger it to close." On `Today`: "keep the today button. If you click it, it should, if you click it and the calendar is already on the month, like if, let's say it's October 2026 and I open the, I'm looking at the calendar and it's showing me October 2026. So today is just one of the days that is there. Uh, in that case, clicking today should change the, should be the same as if you clicked on today for the day. So it should switch the day to today and close the calendar and like refresh, you know, it should show you all today's content. If you're not on today, on the month that it currently is, then it should change the view of the calendar to that month, but leave the calendar open." And: "Esc closes the calendar view without changing the date."
- Q4, the one-day arrows beside the date (owner, 2026-11-10): "But ultimately the arrow should stay either way." On the calendar's own arrows: "when you're looking at the calendar and changing what you're like what date you're picking, those left and right arrows should change one up. It should change the, it should iterate the thing that is one level up from the level of detail you're looking at. So if you're looking at a month with days, the left and right should change the month you're looking at within the same year." Asked whether they cross a year's end: "yes, they do."
- Q5, month totals in a year view (owner, 2026-11-09): "Native month + year (Recommended)". The year view is gone, so it has no totals to show.
- The URL and history on mobile (owner, 2026-11-10): "the calendar shouldn't change the URL or back stack on mobile."
- Ratifying (owner, 2026-11-10, asked whether to ratify the first draft): "skip number four until the design is done". The redraw (owner, 2026-11-16, asked "Should I redraw the calendar in Figma now, to your 2026-11-09 and 11-10 answers including the favorite-team mark?"): "yes, redraw it".

Where the bound comes from: S123 AC 1 bounds the calendar at the first day BT holds games for. That day is the owner's, from the backfill under the **Every game since 1901** feature (FEATURES.md; owner, 2026-11-07, about the backfill, not the calendar): "This goes all the way back to 1901/04/18."

## Open questions

1. **Q6.** **Is the favorite-team mark what you meant: a small red triangle filling the top right corner of each day one of your teams plays?** Your words were "make the top right corner of the days at least one of their teams is playing", so the drawn reading is a 10px corner triangle in Primary Light (Light on the selected red day), shown only when you have at least one favorite team.
