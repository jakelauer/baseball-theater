# A14 — The site footer of links (S141)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A12](A12.md), and through them the footers of the ratified game-shell, game-rooms and standings-search-settings frames. Story **S141**; built by S142. Draws [A13](A13.md)'s Live marker (S147, also a draft) on the footer's bottom line. **Draft, not ratified.**

**Adds:** the site footer (component `site-footer`) at the end of every page the shell draws: a hairline (Light at 12%) across the top, then columns of links, each under a heading (11px IBM Plex Sans Medium, uppercase, muted), each link 13px IBM Plex Sans Regular in Light, then one bottom line holding the "baseball.theater" mark, with A13's Live marker in front of it on a page with an open live WebSocket.
**Overrides:** the one-line footer "Fetched {age} · baseball.theater" (`footer-meta`) on every ratified frame that draws it (the shell-scoreboard frames, game-shell A3, game-rooms A4, shell-scoreboard A4, the Standings frames) and A13's drafts of that line; the offline footer "Offline · saved {age} · baseball.theater" (shell-scoreboard A1, game-rooms A1). Those frames stay sealed and differ from a build only in their footer.
**Repeals:** the "Fetched {age}" label on every page, and the "Offline · saved … ago" footer line (the offline banner keeps the saved copy's time and age).

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/wqGKEFlvyxq0ObWZhRJdPF?node-id=687-12685 — the label "AC — Site footer of links (A14, draft 2026-11-18, awaiting ratification)" on the Shell & navigation page and the frames beneath it, live file as edited on 2026-11-18 (no named version).

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-11-10: *"we need probably a footer like banner kind of section-y thing like most places have where there's a few columns of links and stuff"*; *"I don't think we need the fetched thing at all"*; *"just get rid of the fetched label"*; asked about information on how the site is made, *"just defer this whole idea"*; asked about the footer's policy text, contact and copyright name: *"just links - the story for this just needs to require a ratified list of links and destinations before it can be completed."* On 2026-11-16 (A13): *"Remove the fetch label and replace it with a red dot and Live at the bottom of any page that has an active websocket, with the red dot glowing and slowly blinking"*.

What the draft settles (agent's reading of those words, open to the owner's review):

- **Columns:** two columns of links, "Baseball Theater" and "Privacy", each under its heading, left-aligned with the page's content; 48px apart at mobile, 64px at medium and desktop; 10px between a column's lines; 24px of space above the columns, between them and the bottom line, and below it. Reused: VISUAL-DESIGN's muted meta type (`sm`, muted) for the headings and the mark, Light for the links (a link reads as one by its place in a column, not by colour, as the rail's links do), Light at 12% for the hairline, as the page's other dividers.
- **Mobile:** the columns stay side by side, since two fit in 358px; if the list grows past two columns, they wrap two to a row, a third column under the first. The bottom line always sits under every column.
- **Nothing else:** no copyright line, no year, no text, no logo, no social links: the footer is its columns of links and the bottom line only (agent's reading of *"just links"*, for the owner to confirm, Q1).
- **Where it shows:** at the end of every page the shell draws: the scoreboard, a game page in every room (Videos, Live, Plays, Box, Recap), Standings, Search, Settings (which is signed-in only, ADR-027; standings-search-settings A3) and the change log. Not inside dialogs and drawers (the video player, the nav drawer, the account menu), and not on the account-deleted Settings page, which standings-search-settings A5 draws blank with no footer.
- **Installed app:** shows, the same footer, at the end of the page's scroll, above the home-indicator safe area (A1).
- **Short page:** on a page shorter than the screen the footer sits at the bottom of the screen (the page's content area grows to fill it); on a longer page it follows the content and is reached by scrolling. It is never pinned over content.
- **Fetched:** the "Fetched … ago" line goes from every page; no page shows an age or a fetched time in its footer, so no age is shown anywhere in the footer.
- **Live marker:** on a page with an open live WebSocket the footer opens with A13's `footer-live-strip`, "● Live Data · Active" centred in a full-width band at the very top of the footer, above the hairline and columns, slightly darker than the footer, with nothing beside it, and the footer has no bottom line: "baseball.theater" is dropped there (owner, 2026-11-19: "S147 didn't yet remove the baseball theater link, and the live data bar still shows under the footer"). On every other page there is no strip and the bottom line reads "baseball.theater" alone. A13 owns the marker and its strip (their look, blink, reduced motion, which pages open a socket, reconnecting, polling) and A14 defers to it; A13's "Site footer" decision places the strip at the very top of this footer, as here.
- **Mark:** "baseball.theater" stays as the bottom line's words, muted, on a page with no live WebSocket, and is dropped on a page showing the Live strip. It is the site's name, not a link.
- **Source: MLB:** the recap's "Source: MLB" (game-rooms A2) is the recap's own last line, not part of the footer: it stays where it is, at the end of the recap, above the site footer.
- **Offline:** the footer's "Offline · saved … ago" line (shell-scoreboard A1, game-rooms A1, and the unviewed room's "Offline · baseball.theater") goes: the offline banner already names the saved copy's time and age, so the footer's bottom line reads "baseball.theater" alone while offline (no socket, so no marker). This replaces A13's draft "Offline" decision, which kept that line (Q4). On ratification S74's note (offline, not built) gains the change.
- **Other surfaces:** this one amendment replaces the footer on every surface's frames: game-shell, game-rooms and standings-search-settings get no amendments of their own for it, since the footer is the shell's.
- **Your data:** the link goes to Settings (`/settings`). For a signed-in fan that is the Settings page, whose Account group holds "Download your data" and "Delete account" (standings-search-settings A4, S79). Settings is signed-in only (ADR-027; standings-search-settings A3): signed out, `/settings` sends a visitor to the scoreboard, and no signed-out Settings page is drawn. So as drafted the link shows for a signed-in fan only; for a signed-out visitor it is left out, and with it the "Privacy" column, which holds nothing else (Q5). Sending a signed-out visitor to a sign-in prompt instead would need a state no frame draws yet.
- **No dead links:** "Change log" goes to `/changelog`, which S134 builds (not built yet); on ratification S142's **Depends on** line gains S134, so S142 never ships a link to a route BT doesn't serve. A page sliced on Q2's answer is handled the same way.

## Open questions

1. **Q1.** **Is it right that the footer has no copyright line and no year, only its columns of links, the bottom line "baseball.theater" on a page with no live connection, and A13's Live strip at the top on a live one?** You first asked for a copyright line with the year, then said the footer is *"just links"*. The draft reads that as no copyright line, and `site-footer-mobile`, `site-footer-medium` and `site-footer-desktop` (Figma, Shell & navigation page, under the "AC" label) draw none. Yes: the frames stand. No: a muted "© {year} Baseball Theater" line is added to the bottom line, every frame is redrawn, and S142 gains a test that the year comes from one function given the current time.
2. **Q2.** **Should the footer link a privacy policy, a user agreement or an about page, each then built as its own new story with its text for you to approve, or should those links be left out for now?** You first named a privacy policy, a user agreement and an about page, then said the footer stories draw and build no page of text. BT has none of these pages. As drafted, none is in the list, and the "Privacy" column holds only "Your data". If you want any of them, each becomes its own story (a page of text that waits for your approval of the wording), the link is added to the "Privacy" column (or a new "About" column), and S142 either waits for that story or the page's own story adds its link.
3. **Q3.** **What email address should "Contact" open, and should "Report a problem" go to a new GitHub issue on the baseball-theater repository, or somewhere else (or be dropped)?** The draft's "Baseball Theater" column holds "Change log", "Report a problem" and "Contact". "Contact" is an email link (`mailto:`) and needs the address you want fans to write to; the list can't be ratified with it blank. "Report a problem" is drafted to open `https://github.com/jakelauer/baseball-theater/issues/new` in a new tab, which only works for fans if the repository is public and they have a GitHub account. The other choices: point it at the same email address as Contact, or drop it and keep Contact only.
4. **Q4.** **Should the offline footer's "Offline · saved 18m ago" line go, since the offline banner at the top already says "You're offline. Scores as of 7:42 PM — 18 min ago."?** The draft drops it (`site-footer-offline-mobile`): offline, the footer's bottom line reads "baseball.theater" alone. The Live marker draft (A13) had kept that line, so this answer settles both drafts. Keep it instead: the bottom line reads "Offline · saved 18m ago · baseball.theater" while offline and the offline frame is redrawn.
5. **Q5.** **Should the footer's "Your data" link show only to signed-in fans, or should a signed-out visitor also see it and be taken to sign in?** "Your data" goes to Settings, whose Account group holds "Download your data" and "Delete account". Settings is signed-in only: a signed-out visitor who opens `/settings` is sent to the scoreboard, and no signed-out Settings page exists or is drawn. Every footer frame (Figma, Shell & navigation page, under the "AC" label) shows a signed-in fan. (a) Signed-in only, as drafted: signed out, the link and its "Privacy" column (which holds nothing else unless Q2 adds a policy link) are left out, and a signed-out frame is added before ratifying to show the footer with one column. (b) Signed out, it opens a sign-in prompt and lands on Settings' Account group after sign-in: that prompt is a new state to draw in this amendment before ratifying, and S142 builds it.
6. **Q6.** **Should the footer sit at the bottom of the screen on a page shorter than the screen, as this draft draws, or right after the content, as the Live marker draft (A13) says?** This draft places the footer at the bottom of the screen on a short page (`site-footer-short-desktop`, `site-footer-medium`); A13's "Placement" decision says the footer is at the end of the page's content, not at the bottom of the screen. (The Live marker's place is settled: A13's strip at the very top of the footer, owner, 2026-11-19.) (a) As drafted: A14 wins, and A13 gets a note saying so before it is ratified. (b) Footer right after the content on a short page: `site-footer-short-desktop` and `site-footer-medium` are redrawn with empty space below the footer, and the **Short page** decision is rewritten.
