# A14 — The site footer of links (S141)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A13](A13.md), and through them the footers of the ratified game-shell, game-rooms and standings-search-settings frames. Story **S141**; built by S142, with the About, Privacy policy and Terms of use pages and their links built by S154. Draws [A13](A13.md)'s Live strip (S147, ratified 2026-11-19) at the top of the footer. **Draft, not ratified.**

**Adds:** the site footer (component `site-footer`) at the end of every page the shell draws: on a page with an open live WebSocket, A13's Live strip first; then a hairline (Light at 12%) across the top, columns of links, each under a heading (11px IBM Plex Sans Medium, uppercase, muted), each link 13px IBM Plex Sans Regular in Light, and last a centred copyright line, "© {year} Baseball Theater" (12px IBM Plex Sans Regular, muted).
**Overrides:** the one-line footer "Fetched {age} · baseball.theater" (`footer-meta`) on every ratified frame that draws it (the shell-scoreboard frames, game-shell A3, game-rooms A4, shell-scoreboard A4, the Standings frames), as A13 already overrides it; the offline footer "Offline · saved {age} · baseball.theater" (shell-scoreboard A1, game-rooms A1). Those frames stay sealed and differ from a build only in their footer.
**Repeals:** the "Fetched {age}" label on every page, the "baseball.theater" footer mark, and the "Offline · saved … ago" footer line (the offline banner keeps the saved copy's time and age), as A13 ratified.

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/wqGKEFlvyxq0ObWZhRJdPF?node-id=687-12685 — the label "AC — Site footer of links (A14, draft 2026-11-19, awaiting ratification)" on the Shell & navigation page and the frames beneath it, live file as edited on 2026-11-19 (no named version).

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-11-10: *"we need probably a footer like banner kind of section-y thing like most places have where there's a few columns of links and stuff"*; *"I don't think we need the fetched thing at all"*; *"just get rid of the fetched label"*; asked about information on how the site is made, *"just defer this whole idea"*; asked about the footer's policy text, contact and copyright name: *"just links - the story for this just needs to require a ratified list of links and destinations before it can be completed."* On 2026-11-16 (A13): *"Remove the fetch label and replace it with a red dot and Live at the bottom of any page that has an active websocket, with the red dot glowing and slowly blinking"*. On 2026-11-19, of A13: *"baseball.theater is supposed to be removed, which I've asked for twice."* On 2026-11-19, answering this draft's six questions: *"It should have a copyright"*; *"add one story for all of them together, not one each"*; *"Replace both with a link that goes to https://www.reddit.com/r/BaseballTheater/ and is labelled "Support & Community""*; *"yes"*; *"as drafted"*; *"A13"* (each quoted with its question under Open questions).

What the draft settles (agent's reading of those words, open to the owner's review):

- **One component everywhere:** the footer is one Figma component set, `site-footer` on the Components page, with variants `Live=On|Off` (On: A13's strip opens it) and `Width=Mobile|Wide` (16px or 24px side padding). Every frame in the file that ends in a footer, ratified or draft, on every page, holds an instance of it, so a change to the footer reaches every frame (owner, 2026-11-19: "The footer changes (both of them) aren't updated in all the designs. The footer should be based on modules referenced everywhere so they all update automatically (which is generally how all shared design components should work)"). A frame shows `Live=On` when it draws a game in progress (a "Bot 7"-style inning or the top bar's "LIVE"), since that page holds an open live WebSocket (A13), and `Live=Off` otherwise and offline. Ratified frames' sealed PNGs keep what was ratified; the live file shows the footer as it now stands.
- **Columns:** two columns of links, "Baseball Theater" (About, Change log, Support & Community) and "Legal" (Privacy policy, Terms of use, Your data), each under its heading, left-aligned with the page's content; 48px apart at mobile, 64px at medium and desktop; 10px between a column's lines; 24px of space above the columns, between them and the copyright line, and below it. Which column each new link sits in, the order within a column, and renaming the "Privacy" heading "Legal" (it now holds the terms too) are the agent's reading, for the owner to confirm. Reused: VISUAL-DESIGN's muted meta type (`sm`, muted) for the headings and the copyright line, Light for the links (a link reads as one by its place in a column, not by colour, as the rail's links do), Light at 12% for the hairline, as the page's other dividers.
- **Mobile:** the columns stay side by side, since two fit in 358px; if the list grows past two columns, they wrap two to a row, a third column under the first. The copyright line always sits under every column.
- **Copyright:** the footer's last line reads "© {year} Baseball Theater", centred, muted, where {year} is the current year (owner, 2026-11-19: *"It should have a copyright"*). The wording, "Baseball Theater" as the holder's name, and no "All rights reserved" are the agent's reading, since the owner gave none. S142 takes the year from one named function given the current time (S142 notes, 2026-11-10 evening backlog review).
- **Nothing else:** besides its links, the footer holds the copyright line and, on a live page, A13's strip; no other text, no logo, no social links, no "baseball.theater".
- **Where it shows:** at the end of every page the shell draws: the scoreboard, a game page in every room (Videos, Live, Plays, Box, Recap), Standings, Search, Settings (which is signed-in only, ADR-027; standings-search-settings A3) and the change log, and S154's About, Privacy policy and Terms of use pages. Not inside dialogs and drawers (the video player, the nav drawer, the account menu), and not on the account-deleted Settings page, which standings-search-settings A5 draws blank with no footer.
- **Installed app:** shows, the same footer, at the end of the page's scroll, above the home-indicator safe area (A1).
- **Short page:** the footer sits right after the content, at the end of the page's content, as A13's Placement says (owner, 2026-11-19: *"A13"*); on a page shorter than the screen the leftover height is empty below it. It is never pinned over content or to the screen's bottom edge.
- **Fetched:** the "Fetched … ago" line goes from every page; no page shows an age or a fetched time in its footer, so no age is shown anywhere in the footer.
- **Live marker:** on a page with an open live WebSocket the footer opens with A13's `footer-live-strip`, "● Live Data · Active" centred in a full-width band at the very top of the footer, above the hairline and columns, slightly darker than the footer, with nothing beside it (owner, 2026-11-19: "S147 didn't yet remove the baseball theater link, and the live data bar still shows under the footer"). On every other page there is no strip. A13 owns the marker and its strip (their look, blink, reduced motion, which pages open a socket, reconnecting, polling) and A14 defers to it; A13's "Site footer" decision places the strip at the very top of this footer, as here.
- **Mark:** "baseball.theater" is removed from every footer, live or not, online or offline (owner, 2026-11-19: *"baseball.theater is supposed to be removed, which I've asked for twice."*; ratified in A13). The site's name appears in the footer only in the "Baseball Theater" heading and the copyright line.
- **Source: MLB:** the recap's "Source: MLB" (game-rooms A2) is the recap's own last line, not part of the footer: it stays where it is, at the end of the recap, above the site footer.
- **Offline:** the footer's "Offline · saved … ago" line (shell-scoreboard A1, game-rooms A1, and the unviewed room's "Offline · baseball.theater") goes (owner, 2026-11-19: *"yes"*): the offline banner already names the saved copy's time and age, so offline the footer is its columns and copyright line (no socket, so no strip), as A13's ratified Offline decision says. S74's note (offline, not built) already carries it (2026-11-19 design intake).
- **Other surfaces:** this one amendment replaces the footer on every surface's frames: game-shell, game-rooms and standings-search-settings get no amendments of their own for it, since the footer is the shell's.
- **Your data:** the link goes to Settings (`/settings`). For a signed-in fan that is the Settings page, whose Account group holds "Download your data" and "Delete account" (standings-search-settings A4, S79). Settings is signed-in only (ADR-027; standings-search-settings A3): signed out, `/settings` sends a visitor to the scoreboard. So the link shows for a signed-in fan only; for a signed-out visitor it is left out and the "Legal" column holds Privacy policy and Terms of use (owner, 2026-11-19: *"as drafted"*; `site-footer-signed-out-mobile`).
- **Support & Community:** one link, "Support & Community", replaces the draft's "Report a problem" and "Contact" (owner, 2026-11-19: *"Replace both with a link that goes to https://www.reddit.com/r/BaseballTheater/ and is labelled "Support & Community""*). It leaves BT, so it opens in a new tab (`target="_blank"`, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`), as the list's rule for outside addresses says; it shows no external-link icon, since none of the ratified links draws one (agent's reading).
- **No dead links:** "Change log" goes to `/changelog`, which S134 builds (not built yet); on ratification S142's **Depends on** line gains S134, so S142 never ships a link to a route BT doesn't serve. About (`/about`), Privacy policy (`/privacy`) and Terms of use (`/terms`) are pages BT doesn't have; one story, S154, builds all three (owner, 2026-11-19: *"add one story for all of them together, not one each"*) and its acceptance criteria add their links to the footer (S154 AC 4), so S142 builds the footer without them and S154 adds them. S142's design match shows the footer without those three links until S154 ships.

## Open questions

1. **Q1.** **No copyright line? Your 'just links' read as none.** The draft drew only link columns and a "baseball.theater" bottom line. **Answered 2026-11-19:** *"It should have a copyright"*. Redrawn: the footer ends in a centred, muted "© {year} Baseball Theater" (**Copyright**; wording the agent's reading), and "baseball.theater" is gone from every footer (**Mark**).
2. **Q2.** **Privacy, terms or about pages: add each as its own story, or leave them out (as drafted)?** **Answered 2026-11-19:** *"add one story for all of them together, not one each"*. Sliced as S154; About joins the "Baseball Theater" column and Privacy policy and Terms of use the "Legal" column (**Columns**, **No dead links**).
3. **Q3.** **What email address should 'Contact' open, and where should 'Report a problem' go?** **Answered 2026-11-19:** *"Replace both with a link that goes to https://www.reddit.com/r/BaseballTheater/ and is labelled "Support & Community""*. Redrawn: one "Support & Community" link, opening in a new tab (**Support & Community**).
4. **Q4.** **Offline: drop the footer's 'Offline · saved …' line, since the offline banner already says it?** **Answered 2026-11-19:** *"yes"*. The offline footer is its columns and copyright line (**Offline**, `site-footer-offline-mobile`).
5. **Q5.** **'Your data': signed-in fans only (as drafted), or a sign-in prompt for signed-out visitors?** **Answered 2026-11-19:** *"as drafted"*. Signed out, "Your data" is left out; drawn in `site-footer-signed-out-mobile` (**Your data**).
6. **Q6.** **Layout: left-align the bottom line and pin the footer to the screen's bottom on short pages (as drafted), or follow A13, which centres the line and places the footer right after the content?** **Answered 2026-11-19:** *"A13"*. Redrawn: the copyright line is centred, and on a short page the footer comes right after the content (`site-footer-short-desktop`, `site-footer-medium`; **Short page**).
