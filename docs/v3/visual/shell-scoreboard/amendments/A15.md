# A15 — No off-day mark on the All-Star Game's day (S159, S125)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendment [A8](A8.md) (the calendar date picker, ratified 2026-11-19). Built by S159 (`favoriteTeamsOff`) and S125 (the calendar). **Draft, not ratified.** Its one frame is still to be drawn in Figma before the owner is asked to ratify it; the agent that wrote this file did not edit Figma.

**Adds:** one frame, `scoreboard-calendar-allstar-mobile`, showing the All-Star Game's day with no off-day ✕.
**Overrides:** A8's "Favorite teams' off-day mark" rule (A8, ## Decisions) where it reaches the All-Star Game's day. As A8 words it ("A red ✕ when none of the fan's favorites plays"), that day, whose only game is the All-Star Game between the two league teams, would carry a red ✕ for every fan whose favorites don't play in it, which is every fan. Under this amendment the All-Star Game's day carries no ✕, no tooltip and no tap-and-hold tooltip, like a day with only spring training, exhibition or postseason games: the mark is drawn only on a day with a regular-season game.
**Repeals:** nothing.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-11-21, asked "Once a favorite team's season is over (eliminated, or no postseason), should later days in that same month stop being marked as its off days?": *"Only during the regular season"*. On 2026-11-22, asked "Should the calendar mark the All-Star Game's day as an off day for favorite teams not playing in it?", the owner chose *"No mark on the All-Star day (Recommended)"*; that option read: *"keep your "Only during the regular season" answer literal; the All-Star day looks like spring training and postseason-only days. A8 gets a small amendment for you to ratify, since its frame draws that day with a red ✕."*

## Interpretation

- *Agent:* the All-Star Game is not a regular-season game (MLB's `gameType` `A`), so its day is a day without a regular-season game, which S159's `favoriteTeamsOff` answers `null` for (S167's `regularSeason` is `false` that day). The mark follows that answer; nothing in the view special-cases the All-Star Game.
- *Agent:* the option's words say A8's frame draws the All-Star day with a red ✕. Read at this draft, A8's sealed frames draw September 2026 only, so the red ✕ there is A8's written rule as it applies to that day, not a drawn frame; this amendment adds the frame that shows the day unmarked.
- *Agent:* the day's game count still shows (`1 gm`), and the day is pickable and not dimmed, since it has a game; only the ✕ is gone.
