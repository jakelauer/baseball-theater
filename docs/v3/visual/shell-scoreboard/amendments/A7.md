# A7 — The Win Probability slot on live and final games (S107)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A6](A6.md). Story **S107**; built by S108. Ratified 2026-11-19.

**Adds:** the Win Probability slot occupant, when the reader's Game-Row Slot is Win Probability, on a live row (the line so far, with the away team and its current chance at the top right and the home team and its chance at the bottom right) and a final row (the whole game's line, with the away team at the top right and the home team at the bottom right); on a scheduled row (a game that hasn't started) no slot box is drawn at all, whichever occupant the reader chose; with Hide Scores on, a live or final row's occupant keeps the two teams, shows "–" in place of a live chance, and reads "Hidden with scores" where the line was.
**Overrides:** nothing.
**Repeals:** the pre-game win probability bar on the scheduled row (DESIGN.md: "win probability (scheduled row)"): no feed gives pre-game odds. A scheduled row draws no slot box in its place, with Win Probability or the default Status occupant chosen.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-10-30, told that the frames draw win probability only before first pitch and no feed gives pre-game odds, the owner said: "It is actually least useful before the game. During and after is definitely useful."

- Win probability shows during and after the game, from S93's play metrics (`winProbabilitySeries`, S106).
- The pre-game bar is repealed.

On 2026-11-10, reviewing the first draft of section W (not a ratification), the owner said: "Remove the inning/status text from the bottom right. Move the bottom team's text to the right. Remove the WIN PROB text from the top left. The bottom team should always be the home team. Top team should not be bold/gold." On 2026-11-16 the owner said the draft should be redrawn to it: "you should redraw anything that has un-redrawn changes".

- No inning or status text at the bottom right (no `Final`, no `Bot 7`).
- No `WIN PROB` label at the top left.
- The bottom team is always the home team, and its text sits on the right.
- The top team (the away team) is neither bold nor gold.

On 2026-11-16 the owner answered the draft's three open questions. Asked whether the drawn Hide Scores occupant was right (both teams kept, `–` for each live chance, `Hidden with scores` where the line was), the owner said: "correct". Asked whether a scheduled row should read `Odds at first pitch` or fall back to another occupant before the game, and what the default Status slot's scheduled row should show with the pre-game bar repealed, the owner said: "Remove the box entirely."

- With Hide Scores on, a live or final row's occupant keeps both team names in their places, shows `–` for each live chance, and reads `Hidden with scores` where the line was.
- A game that hasn't started draws no slot box at all, whichever occupant the reader chose (Win Probability or the default Status slot); there is no `Odds at first pitch` occupant.

## Choices made while drawing (accepted at ratification)

- The occupant keeps the slot's size and border; the line, area and dashed 50% line are the Live room's Win probability card (S106) in small.
- With the away team at the top and the home team at the bottom, the line rises toward the away team and falls toward the home team as each one's chance grows (the line is the away team's chance; equivalently, the home team's chance read from the bottom). The Live room's card draws the home team's chance rising, so the two read in opposite directions; the owner may prefer the line flipped back with the labels.
- Both team labels use the same muted style (cream at 50%, SemiBold 9): with the top team no longer bold or gold, neither team stands out, and the line shows who leads.
- A live row shows each team's current chance beside its name (`LAD 78%`, `SD 22%`); a final row shows only the names (a percentage would read 100% or 0%).
- With no slot box, a scheduled row keeps its other content where it was (time and venue, teams, probable pitchers); the space the box took stays empty rather than the row narrowing.
