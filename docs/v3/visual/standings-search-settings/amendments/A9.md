# A9 — Standings' three views (S129)

Amends the ratified [standings-search-settings design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A8](A8.md). Story **S129**; built by S130 (from S139's `phases` and S140's `champion`). **Draft, not ratified.** Redrawn on 2026-11-16 to the owner's answers of 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-13 (the first draft, of 2026-11-09, drew a single spring training label).

**Adds:** a view switch under the page heading that chooses spring training, the regular season or the playoffs; the "as of" words beside the heading on a day that isn't today; the spring table without its games-behind column; the regular season before its first game; the playoffs list; the first spring morning with nothing to show; and the World Series box.
**Overrides:** the page heading and the area above the tables in `standings-mobile`, `standings-medium` and `standings-desktop`; on mobile only, A7's content-width tables, which become full width at a minimum (owner, 2026-11-10: "Make the standings full width on mobile at a minimum"). A7's content-width tables still hold on medium and desktop, and A7's abbreviations still hold on mobile.
**Repeals:** nothing.

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/wqGKEFlvyxq0ObWZhRJdPF?node-id=588-18813 — Standings page, section "Y — Standings' three views (S129, awaiting ratification)", live file as edited in the S129 sessions (no named version).

### The view switch

- It is the game page's ratified tab row (`room-tabs`, shell-scoreboard), reused: text tabs in Plex Medium 14px, the shown view in Bold with the 16×2px brand underline, a hairline under the row. Its tabs sit at the start of the row 24px apart (the game page spreads its five tabs across the row; two or three tabs spread that way would drift apart), and the row is as wide as the content column.
- It sits under the heading, 24px below it, and above the World Series box and the tables.
- Its tabs are always in the order **Spring training**, **Regular season**, **Playoffs**, and it shows the tabs the day offers (S130 AC 1), the shown view marked. The sets drawn: spring training and the regular season (`standings-spring`, `standings-spring-empty`, `standings-regular-blank`); the regular season and the playoffs (`standings-playoffs-champion`, `standings-regular-champion`). On a day that offers all three, all three show, in that order.
- With the regular season alone on offer the switch is not shown at all, since there is nothing to choose (`standings-offseason`); the page is then the regular season's.
- Choosing a tab changes the view in place; the table on screen stays until the new one arrives (S130 AC 1).

### Spring training

- The spring table has W, L and PCT and no games-behind column (owner, 2026-11-10: "There, just hide that. Just don't show the column."); the header loses `GB` too. MLB's spring PCT is shown as served; tied spring games count in neither W nor L.
- **Opening day's morning**, before the first regular-season game is final, spring training opens first with spring's last table (S128's own table is spring's then), so the page looks like `standings-spring` with spring's final records, and the regular season is the blank list of `standings-regular-blank`.

### The regular season before its first game

The new season's teams in their divisions, in name order, each `0` and `0`, with PCT and GB `—` (S139 AC 4 serves no other figure), drawn in the regular season's ordinary table, games-behind column included. It is never last season's final table (owner, 2026-11-13, asked which: "new season's empty standings.").

### The playoffs

The teams that have clinched a playoff spot, with no numbers (owner, 2026-11-10: "it'll just be a list of teams with no with no numbers"): grouped by division in the regular season's division order, each division's teams in the regular season's row order, a division with no clinched team left out. Each group keeps its division heading and the `TEAM` header, and each row is the team's swatch and name (its abbreviation on mobile, name and abbreviation on medium and desktop, as A7 draws them). What it shows once playoff games are played is not designed here (owner, 2026-11-10: "We'll figure that out later.").

### The "as of" line

- On a day that isn't today the heading reads "Standings as of March 2, 2027": `Standings` in the title style, then "as of March 2, 2027" beside it on the same baseline in Plex Regular 20px (18px on mobile) at the muted 50%, wrapping under the title when the screen is too narrow. Today, or no day in the address, it is just `Standings`.
- The day is the day being viewed (owner, 2026-11-10: "It's the day you're viewing."), written as the full month, the day and the year, read as a calendar day so it never shifts with the reader's time zone.
- It is the same in every view: the switch, not the heading, says which view is shown (owner, 2026-11-10: "it should show standings as of March 2, 2027, and then it should show spring training as the standings.").
- On a day whose table is a season's final one it still names the day being viewed, not the table's own date (a final table's date is its season's last day): on December 1, 2026 it reads "Standings as of December 1, 2026" (`standings-offseason`).

### The World Series box

- **What it is.** A box with a 12px radius on the raised ground, a 2px gold border that runs from Accent (`#F5AD1D`) through a pale gold highlight and back, and a soft gold glow around it: the eyebrow "2026 World Series champions" (Plex SemiBold 11px, upper case, Accent), the winner's swatch and name in Plex SemiBold 24px (22px on mobile) filled with the same gold gradient, the muted line "Won the World Series on October 28, 2026", and, while the playoffs view is offered and isn't the view shown, the link "View the playoffs →" in the brand text colour (owner, 2026-11-10: "it should have some fancy like gold effect border around it or something. It should just have some like cool, shiny, fancy looking effect, text and stuff, and it should be celebratory and special effect, spec ops kind of thing."). It is 420px wide on medium and desktop and full width on mobile.
- **Where.** Under the switch (under the heading when there is no switch), above the tables.
- **When it shows.** From the day after the last World Series game (owner, 2026-11-13: "option a"). In that first week, through the seventh day after the last game, the playoffs view opens first (S130 AC 1) and the box shows above both views: above the playoffs with no link, since the fan is already there (`standings-playoffs-champion`), and above the regular season with its link (`standings-regular-champion`). From the eighth day the playoffs view is gone, the regular season opens first and alone, and the box stays without the link (`standings-offseason`).
- **How long it stays** is still open (Q7): drawn as the agent's proposal, through the day before spring training's first day, so it is gone the morning Standings turns to spring training.
- **What moves.** When the box first appears, one gold sheen sweeps once across its border and the winner's name, left to right, in about 1.2 seconds on Material's standard curve (`cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`), and then everything holds still: no loop, no pulse. A fan whose device asks for reduced motion gets no sheen: the box is drawn still, exactly as in the frames.

### The heading and the browser tab

The heading screen readers hear is the visible one. The exact text:

| Day and view | Heading | Browser tab |
| ------------ | ------- | ----------- |
| Today, spring training | Standings | Standings · Spring training · Baseball Theater |
| Today, the regular season (with or without the switch) | Standings | Standings · Regular season · Baseball Theater |
| Today, the playoffs | Standings | Standings · Playoffs · Baseball Theater |
| March 2, 2027 (not today), spring training | Standings as of March 2, 2027 | Standings as of March 2, 2027 · Spring training · Baseball Theater |
| December 1, 2026 (not today), the regular season | Standings as of December 1, 2026 | Standings as of December 1, 2026 · Regular season · Baseball Theater |

Only Standings sets the tab's title (owner, 2026-11-10: "just keep it scoped to the standings page for now"); every other page keeps "Baseball Theater".

### The off-season's final table

Drawn as the ratified frames draw it (with A7), unmarked, in `standings-regular-champion` and `standings-offseason`; whether it gets a mark is still open (Q2).

### The first day of spring training

On the first day of spring training, before its first game, Standings shows spring training, marked in the switch, with "Spring training starts today" and its muted line in place of the tables, and the switch still offers the regular season (`standings-spring-empty`). The day before shows the prior season's final standings, as `standings-offseason` draws them (owner, 2026-11-10: "spring training standings should show on that day. The day before should show the prior season's standings.").

### Width on mobile

On mobile every table, the switch and the box are full width at a minimum (owner, 2026-11-10: "Make the standings full width on mobile at a minimum"), overriding A7's content-width tables at mobile only; a table wider than the screen still scrolls inside its own box. Medium and desktop keep A7.

### Loading, empty and error

- The first load is the ratified loading state (`ViewState`), with no switch and no box, since nothing is known yet; the heading shows as it will.
- Switching views never shows the loading state: the table on screen stays until the new one is drawn (S130 AC 1).
- The error state is the ratified one, with no switch and no box.
- The ratified empty state, "No standings for this date.", stays for a regular-season or off-season answer with no divisions. Spring training with nothing to show is `standings-spring-empty`, not that state, and keeps the switch.

- Q5, the "as of" line in the regular season (owner, 2026-11-10, asked whether regular-season standings show it too): "Yes, if you're not on the today. But if you're not on today's date, and it should follow the rules I already said for the format."
- Q6, the heading and the browser tab (owner, 2026-11-10, asked whether they should say spring training too): "The title of the page should just show the standings, uh, and if the date, if there's a like a day that's been picked, it should show standings as of that day. ## Interpretation

- *Agent:* the switch reuses the game page's ratified tab row rather than the settings' segmented choices: it already marks one of several views of one page, and a fan who has used a game page knows it.
- *Agent:* the switch is hidden when only the regular season is on offer: a single tab chooses nothing.
- *Agent:* the visible heading stays `Standings` (with "as of …" on another day) in every view, and the browser tab adds the view's name; the owner's "If you're in spring training, it should say spring training" is met by the marked tab on the page and by the tab title, and his later "it should show spring training as the standings" by the switch.
- *Agent:* on the playoffs view the box drops its link, since the switch shows the fan is already there; S130 AC 4's test of the box "with its link" in that week is then on the regular season view.
- *Agent:* the gold is the brand's Accent (`#F5AD1D`) with a paler gold highlight for the sheen; the gradient and the glow are this box's alone, not a new token, and the winner's swatch is the team's data colour, as everywhere else.
- *Agent:* the frames keep the ratified frames' sample teams (the AL, with "Oakland Athletics" and `OAK`) and show the 2026 winner as the Milwaukee Brewers, as S140's recording has it; S130 shows what the answer says.
- *Agent:* the desktop frames have no wordmark in the top bar over the page; the rail's wordmark is the only one, and the top bar keeps its menu and account controls where they were. This follows the owner's shell-scoreboard A11 decision on issue #38's "no logo in the desktop header" (owner, 2026-11-15: "Polish looks good. Yes amend them and ratify.").
- *Agent:* each frame ends below its content (the footer); the first spring morning and the playoffs list are shorter pages than the ratified frames' height.
- *Agent:* on mobile the playoffs list keeps A7's abbreviations, though with no numbers a full name would fit, so the mobile page reads the same in every view.

## Open questions

- **Q2.** **Should the off-season's final table be marked as final?** From the day after the regular season ends until spring training starts, the regular season view shows the season's final table (S128's `phase: "final"`); as drawn it looks exactly like a mid-season table, and only the World Series box (after the Series) and the "as of" words (on a past day) say anything about it. If yes, a mark is drawn for it (for example a muted "Final" beside the switch, or "2026 regular season · final" under the heading), with frames of their own, and FEATURES records your words; if no, it stays as drawn and nothing changes.
- **Q7.** **How long should the World Series box stay: until spring training starts, or something else?** As drawn it shows from the day after the last World Series game and stays, without its link from the eighth day, through the day before spring training's first day, so it is gone the morning Standings turns to spring training (the agent's proposal; the earlier wording was "until spring training's first game", which ends on the same day now that spring training shows from its first morning). If you accept it, S130 and S140 build it as written; if you want it to go sooner (say after a month) or to stay into spring training, S130's rule for when it shows changes, and S140's champion, which is served only on off-season days, changes too before either ships.
