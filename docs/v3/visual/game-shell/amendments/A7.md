# A7 — A status marker says when a game is suspended or delayed (S157)

Amends the ratified [game-shell design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A6](A6.md), and, for the scoreboard's game rows, the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../../shell-scoreboard/DESIGN.md) and its amendments. Story **S157**; a build story, added once this amendment is ratified, builds it. **Draft, not ratified.** Nothing here is ratified until the owner says so; until then the frames and the component are a proposal.

**Adds:** the `game-status-marker` component set (Components page): the dot and label that say a game's status, in four states, `State=Live`, `State=Delayed`, `State=Delayed start` and `State=Suspended`, each in two places, `Place=Top bar` (inside the `game-topbar`) and `Place=Row` (the top-left of a scoreboard game row, the `Rich Scoreboard Module`). Every label but "LIVE" is sentence case (owner, 2026-11-22, Q6 and Q8). A delayed game reads "● Delayed · Rain" in the top bar and "● Delayed · Bot 3" on its row, with a steady `Accent` dot; a game whose start is delayed reads "● Delayed start · Rain" in its pre-game top bar and "● Delayed start · Rain" on its row, with the same steady `Accent` dot; a suspended game reads "○ Suspended · Rain" in the top bar and "○ Suspended · Rain" on its row, with a steady `Accent` ring. On a phone, a delayed, delayed-start or suspended game's marker sits on its own line under the team names (owner, 2026-11-22, Q5), while a game in progress keeps "● LIVE" beside the teams (owner, 2026-11-22, Q7): the `game-topbar` component now wraps, and holds a `status-line` (hidden unless a phone shows one of those three states) with a marker instance, below `NYY @ BOS`.
**Overrides:** the live indicator drawn in the `game-topbar` ("● LIVE", S36 R6) and the live row's status ("● Bot 7", shell-scoreboard frames): both were copies of one dot and label, and both are now instances of `game-status-marker` at `State=Live`, which looks exactly as they did (an `Accent 2` dot, blinking, and its label). Neither is shown for a delayed or suspended game any more: the marker's other states replace them. Also overrides game-shell DESIGN's "No status" in the top bar for one pre-game case: a game whose start is delayed shows the marker before first pitch (owner, 2026-11-22, Q1); the `Rich Scoreboard Module`'s scheduled row holds a hidden `State=Delayed start` instance in its `status-group`, shown in place of the start time for such a game.
**Repeals:** nothing.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On #79, 2026-11-20, of suspended games: "It should look like a live game in the UI in that the user should be able to see what happened so far in the game, but it should be clear to the user that the game is suspended and polling should continue for up to 6 hours." Asked how to handle it, the owner chose "Fix now, design after (Recommended)", offered as "A design story then draws a status label for suspended (and delayed) games for you to ratify". On #72, 2026-11-17, of rain delays: "Yes, a rain delay should show the live tab." On 2026-11-22, shown this draft's open questions, the owner answered Q1, Q3 and Q4 (quoted there); later the same day, shown screenshots of the redrawn frames, Q2, Q5 and Q6, and then, shown the phone top bars and the states image, Q7 and Q8 (all quoted there).

What the draft proposes (agent's reading of those words, for the owner to ratify or change):

- **Marker:** a dot and a label, as the ratified live indicator is: an 8px dot, then 6px, then the label in 12px IBM Plex Sans Bold, in the dot's colour, no pill and no background (VISUAL-DESIGN's live role). In the top bar "LIVE" stays upper case, as ratified, and the other three are sentence case, so they read alike: "Delayed · Rain", "Delayed start · Rain", "Suspended · Rain" (owner, 2026-11-22, Q6 and Q8); on a row every label is sentence case, as "Bot 7" is. Delayed, delayed start and suspended use `Accent` gold, a dot for a delay and a ring for a suspension, as drawn (owner, 2026-11-22, Q2).
- **One component:** `game-status-marker` is one component set with a variant per state and place, and every frame places an instance of it: the `game-topbar` component's `live-indicator` and the `Rich Scoreboard Module`'s live `status-group` now each hold an instance (`State=Live`), so the ratified frames look as they did, and a change to the marker reaches every frame. Before this the dot and label were drawn twice, as copies (the top bar's "● LIVE" and the row's "● Bot 7"); they were alike but for their words, so nothing had to be chosen between them.
- **Codes:** `State=Live` is a game in progress: MLB `I` with no delay reason (`I` "In Progress", `IH` "Instant Replay"), a manager challenge `M` and an umpire review `N`. `State=Delayed` is an in-game delay: MLB `I` whose detailed state starts "Delayed" (`IR` "Delayed: Rain", and `I9`, `IA`–`IG`, `II`, `IL`, `IO`, `IP`, `IS`, `IT`, `IV`, `IY`, `IZ`); its label names MLB's reason after the dot ("Delayed · Rain"; plain "Delayed" for `IO`, which gives none). `State=Delayed start` is a start delayed before first pitch: MLB `P` whose detailed state starts "Delayed Start" (`PR` "Delayed Start: Rain", and `P9`, `PA`–`PG`, `PI`, `PL`, `PO`, `PP`, `PS`, `PV`, `PY`), its label naming MLB's reason ("Delayed start · Rain"; plain "Delayed start" for `PO`, which gives none); `P` "Pre-Game" and `PW` "Warmup" get no marker (owner, 2026-11-22, Q1). `State=Suspended` is MLB's `T` and `U`, every code, its label naming MLB's reason after the dot ("Suspended · Rain" for `TR`/`UR`; plain "Suspended" for `T`, `TO`, `U` and `UO`, which give none; owner, 2026-11-22, Q3) (B81's code list, `packages/domain/src/window.ts`).
- **Top bar:** the marker takes the live indicator's place, after `NYY @ BOS` (on a phone, a delayed, delayed-start or suspended marker goes on its own line under the team names instead, left-aligned with `NYY`, owner, 2026-11-22, Q5; a game in progress keeps "● LIVE" beside the teams on a phone too, as ratified, owner, 2026-11-22, Q7), for a game in progress, delayed or suspended; a game whose start is delayed shows it before first pitch, with the pre-game tabs and no Live room; any other scheduled game, and a final one, shows none, as ratified (game-shell DESIGN, "No status"). The Live tab stays for a game in progress, delayed or suspended, so the play so far stays visible (owner, 2026-11-20).
- **Scoreboard row:** the marker takes the status place at the top-left of the row. A delayed game keeps the inning after the state ("Delayed · Bot 3"); a suspended game names the reason instead ("Suspended · Rain", owner, 2026-11-22, Q3); the score, the team rows and the slot stay as for a live game. A delayed start replaces the start time ("Delayed start · Rain · Citi Field") and the row otherwise stays a scheduled row (probable pitchers, win probability).
- **Motion:** only `State=Live` blinks (VISUAL-DESIGN's slow live blink); delayed, delayed start and suspended are steady, since nothing is happening, and so need nothing under reduced motion.
- **Resumed:** once a suspended or delayed game resumes (MLB reports it `I` again with no delay reason), the marker goes back to `State=Live`, "● LIVE" and "● Bot 7", blinking, with nothing to say it was stopped, and once it ends it reads Final like any other game (owner, 2026-11-22, Q4). A delayed start that begins goes to `State=Live` the same way. A suspended game that BT stops polling (6 hours after it saw it turn suspended, B81) keeps "○ Suspended · …" from its last stored status until a schedule read sees it resume or end. A suspended game that resumes on a later day reads "● LIVE" on that day; the earlier day's row reads whatever BT last stored for it.
- **Footer:** unchanged: A13's "● Live Data · Active" strip follows the page's live connection, not the game's state, so a delayed, delayed-start or suspended game whose page is still connected shows it (drawn so in the game frames).

## Open questions

1. **Q1.** **Should a game whose start is delayed (MLB `P`, e.g. "Delayed Start: Rain", before first pitch) show the marker too, or stay a plain scheduled game until it starts?** A delayed start has no Live room (it is pre-game) and today reads as scheduled. **Answered 2026-11-22:** *"Show “● Delayed start · Rain”"* (the option read: on its row and top bar, in the delay's gold, adding a frame). Redrawn 2026-11-22: `State=Delayed start` added to `game-status-marker`, the `status-marker-game-delayed-start-*` frames added, and a delayed-start row added to `status-marker-scoreboard-*`.
2. **Q2.** **Are a steady gold dot for a delay and a steady gold ring for a suspension clear enough, or should they use other colours or shapes?** As drawn (`game-status-marker`, Components page), delayed, delayed start and suspended all use `Accent` (#F5AD1D), the warm highlight, so none reads as live green or as the red of strikes; the ring marks a suspension as more stopped than a delay. **Answered 2026-11-22:** *"Yes, as drawn (Recommended)"*. Nothing to redraw.
3. **Q3.** **Should the suspended label name MLB's reason too ("SUSPENDED · RAIN"), as the delay label does, or stay "SUSPENDED"?** MLB gives a reason for most suspensions (`TR` "Suspended: Rain", …). **Answered 2026-11-22:** *"Name the reason"* (the option read: "SUSPENDED · RAIN" on the top bar, "Suspended · Rain" on the row). Redrawn 2026-11-22: the suspended variants read "SUSPENDED · RAIN" and "Suspended · Rain".
4. **Q4.** **Once a suspended game has resumed and finished, should anything say it was suspended (for example on the earlier day's scoreboard row), or is a plain Final enough?** **Answered 2026-11-22:** *"Plain Final (Recommended)"*. Nothing to redraw: a resumed game reads "● LIVE" and then Final like any other (Resumed, above).
5. **Q5.** **On a phone, the longer top-bar labels no longer fit beside the date: which should give way?** At 390px "○ SUSPENDED · RAIN" touched the date and "● DELAYED START · RAIN" overlapped it. **Answered 2026-11-22:** *"Marker on its own line"* (the option read: under the team names, on a phone). Redrawn 2026-11-22: the `game-topbar` component wraps and holds a hidden `status-line` with a marker instance; the delayed, delayed-start and suspended phone frames show it under `NYY @ BOS`, the top bar 80px tall.
6. **Q6.** **In the top bar, should a delayed start read "● DELAYED START · RAIN" in upper case, like LIVE, DELAYED and SUSPENDED there? (The row stays "Delayed start · Rain".)** **Answered 2026-11-22:** *"Sentence case everywhere"* (the option read: "● Delayed start · Rain" in the top bar too). Redrawn 2026-11-22: `Place=Top bar, State=Delayed start` reads "Delayed start · Rain".
7. **Q7.** **On a phone, should a game in progress's "● LIVE" also move to its own line under the team names, like the delay and suspension markers, or stay beside the teams as ratified?** Your Q5 answer was about the longer labels that didn't fit; "● LIVE" fits beside the teams. **Answered 2026-11-22:** *"Keep LIVE beside the teams (Recommended)"*. Nothing to redraw: `status-marker-game-live-mobile` already shows it there.
8. **Q8.** **Should DELAYED and SUSPENDED in the top bar become sentence case too ("● Delayed · Rain", "○ Suspended · Rain"), to match the delayed start, or stay upper case like "LIVE"?** **Answered 2026-11-22:** *"Sentence case for all three (Recommended)"* (the option read: "Delayed, Delayed start and Suspended read alike; LIVE stays upper case."). Redrawn 2026-11-22: `Place=Top bar, State=Delayed` reads "Delayed · Rain" and `Place=Top bar, State=Suspended` reads "Suspended · Rain"; `status-marker-states` and the delayed and suspended game frames re-exported.
