# A6 — A video's play link moves onto its meta line, as a red "Play" (S144)

**Draft — not yet ratified.** Shown to the owner for ratification; S145 builds it once ratified.

Amends the ratified [game-shell design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A5](A5.md). Story **S144**; built by S145.

**Overrides:** A5's play link on the Videos room's cards and list rows. A clip with a play no longer has `Play · Top 6th →` on a line of its own under the meta line: the meta line itself starts with "Play" in red, in place of "Highlight", and that word is the link to the play.
**Keeps:** A5's link in the floating player's overlay row, and every clip with no play (`Highlight · Sep 21, 2026`, `Condensed game · …`, `Recap · …`).
**Repeals:** the separate play-link line under a card's meta line.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. Looking at section V on 2026-11-10 the owner said: "Move hte play link to the 2nd line. Replace "Highlight" with "Play" in red. If there's no play, just keep "Highlight"".

The bullets below are the choices made while drawing, offered with the draft; they become the owner's with the ratification.

- **Link:** the word "Play" alone is the link to the play (A5's destination: the Plays room opened on that play). The rest of the line is plain meta text, not part of the link.
- **Rest of the line:** `· Sep 21, 2026`, the date as today, in the meta line's muted text. A5's inning (`Top 6th`) and arrow are dropped, so the line stays as short as today's and fits a two-column mobile card on one line.
- **Red:** Primary Light (`#FF5247`), VISUAL-DESIGN's red for text on Dark, the same colour variable A5's link uses; the rest of the line keeps the meta line's muted Light. No new colour. "Play" takes the meta line's own size and weight (not A5's link size), so only its colour sets it apart.
- **Hover and focus:** on hover (a pointer over the word) "Play" is underlined and the card's still does not zoom; hovering anywhere else on the card zooms the still as today. Keyboard focus reaches the card first, then "Play", which shows the theme's focus ring around the word alone.
- **Other kinds:** whenever a clip has a play the line starts with "Play", whatever its kind: a `Video`-kind clip or one with no kind reads `Play · Sep 21, 2026` too. A clip's play always wins: a recap or condensed clip that has a play reads `Play · …` too (today none does).
- **No date:** a clip with a play and no date reads `Play` alone, so the meta line is drawn whenever the clip has a play. A clip with neither a play, a kind nor a date still has no meta line, as today.
- **No play:** a clip with no play keeps `Highlight · Sep 5, 2026` exactly as drawn today (muted, not a link; Sep 5 is the format's example, the frames show Sep 21); a recap or condensed game keeps its own word (`Recap · …`, `Condensed game · …`).
- **Rest of the card:** unchanged: the 16:9 still and its duration badge, the title, and that choosing the card anywhere but "Play" plays the clip. Since "Play" now sits inside the card, the meta line can no longer sit inside the one button that plays the clip (today `VideoCard.tsx` makes the whole card one button, and a link inside a button would also start the clip): S145 keeps the link out of that button, never nested in it, while the rest of the card still plays the clip. Hide Scores doesn't change the line (A5).
- **Touch:** tapping the word "Play" opens the play; tapping anywhere else on the card plays the clip. The word's tap area is at least 24×24 px (padded around the word, not drawn), so a thumb can land on it without starting the clip.
- **Where else:** only the game's Videos room cards and list rows. The floating player's overlay row keeps A5's `Play · Top 6th →` link (the player has no meta line to put it on), and Search's cards are unchanged (they have no play link; S127 designs Search's player).
