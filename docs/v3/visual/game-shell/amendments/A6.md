# A6 — A video's play link becomes an inning pill after the date (S144)

**Draft — not yet ratified.** Shown to the owner for ratification; S145 builds it once ratified.

Amends the ratified [game-shell design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A5](A5.md), and, for Search's result rows and Search's floating player, the standings-search-settings surface's Search frames. Story **S144**; built by S145 (the game's Videos room and the shared floating player) and by the stories that build Search's results and Search's player (S5, and S127's build).

**Adds:** the `play-inning-pill` component: the play's half-inning, `Top 6th` or `Bot 3rd` (the words `atBatInningLabel` already gives, 11px IBM Plex Sans Medium, in the date's muted text colour), in a fully rounded pill filled with `color/bg/raised` (#1C1A19), a shade off the page and card background, 2px above and below and 8px each side. It is the link to the clip's play.
**Overrides:** A5's play link everywhere it is drawn. On the Videos room's cards and list rows a clip with a play no longer has `Play · Top 6th →` on a line of its own: its meta line reads the date, then the pill inline after it (`Sep 21, 2026` [Top 6th]). In the floating player's overlay row the pill replaces `Play · Top 6th →`. In Search's floating player the pill replaces the At-Bat link S127's draft gave it, after "Go to: Game →". On Search's result rows a result with a play gets the same meta line as a card.
**Repeals:** the separate play-link line under a card's meta line, the "Play ·" prefix and arrow in the play link's words, the link's red, and this draft's earlier red "Play" in place of "Highlight" and red "Inspect play" pill.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. Looking at section V on 2026-11-10 the owner said: "Move hte play link to the 2nd line. Replace "Highlight" with "Play" in red. If there's no play, just keep "Highlight"". Shown this draft on 2026-11-19: "S144: move the link after the date, put the link in a pill inline, and change it to "inspect play"". Asked, the same day, whether a play's line keeps "Highlight ·" before the date (it pushed the pill onto a second line on the two-column mobile grid) or drops it so the pill fits inline: "Drop "Highlight ·"". Asked whether the pill stays on the Videos room's cards or also reaches the floating player and Search's cards: "Player and Search". Asked whether, in Search's floating player, the pill replaces S127's "At-Bat →" after "Go to: Game →": "Game link + pill". Shown the pill the same day: "The pill background should be subtly different from the background. Actually, instead of inspect play, use Top/Bot # The pill text should be the same color as the date".

The bullets below are the choices made while drawing, offered with the draft; they become the owner's with the ratification.

- **Link:** the pill alone is the link to the play (A5's destination: the Plays room opened on that play); the date before it is plain meta text, not part of the link.
- **Rest of the line:** a clip with a play reads its date, `Sep 21, 2026`, in the meta line's muted text, then the pill 6px after it on the same line, holding the play's half-inning as A5's link named it (`Top 6th`, `Bot 3rd`). Its kind word ("Highlight") is dropped, so the date and pill fit a two-column mobile card on one line. A5's "Play ·" and arrow are dropped.
- **Colour:** no red. The pill's words take the date's colour (`color/text/muted`: Light at 50%) and its fill is `color/bg/raised` (#1C1A19), a shade lighter than the Dark page and card it sits on, so it reads as a quiet chip. No new colour.
- **Hover and focus:** on hover (a pointer over the pill) its fill lightens to `color/bg/raised-current` (#272422) and the card's still does not zoom; hovering anywhere else on the card zooms the still as today. Keyboard focus reaches the card first, then the pill, which shows the theme's focus ring around the pill alone.
- **Other kinds:** whenever a clip has a play its line is the date and the pill, whatever its kind: a `Video`-kind clip or one with no kind (missing `kind`) reads `Sep 21, 2026` [Top 6th] too. A clip's play always wins: a recap or condensed clip that has a play reads the same (today none does).
- **No date:** a clip with a play and no date shows the pill alone on its meta line, so the meta line is drawn whenever the clip has a play. A clip with neither a play, a kind nor a date still has no meta line, as today.
- **No play:** a clip with no play keeps `Highlight · Sep 5, 2026` exactly as drawn today (muted, not a link, no pill; Sep 5 is the format's example, the frames show Sep 21); a recap or condensed game keeps its own word (`Recap · …`, `Condensed game · …`).
- **Rest of the card:** unchanged: the 16:9 still and its duration badge, the title, and that choosing the card anywhere but the pill plays the clip. Since the pill sits inside the card, the meta line can no longer sit inside the one button that plays the clip (today `VideoCard.tsx` makes the whole card one button, and a link inside a button would also start the clip): S145 keeps the pill out of that button, never nested in it, while the rest of the card still plays the clip. Hide Scores doesn't change the line (A5).
- **Touch:** tapping the pill opens the play; tapping anywhere else on the card plays the clip. The pill's tap area is at least 24×24 px (padded around the pill, not drawn), so a thumb can land on it without starting the clip.
- **Where else:** the floating player's overlay row shows the pill where A5's `Play · Top 6th →` was, opening the play and closing the player as that link did. In Search's floating player it follows "Go to: Game →" in place of the At-Bat link S127's draft gave it (S127 draws that player). Search's result rows show the same date-and-pill line for a result with a play (S5 builds the results).
