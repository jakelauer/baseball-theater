# A7 — Equal, centered room tabs; Plays opens with no at-bat expanded (issue #38)

Amends the ratified [game-rooms design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A6](A6.md). Built by pull request #58 (issue #38, the owner's site-polish list), shipped in v3.2.1. A written amendment: the owner's directions, ratified as words (no Figma frames). The v3.2.1 build is its drawn record.

**Adds:** nothing.
**Overrides:** the room tabs on every room frame, including A2's full-width tabs on Live (`live-tabs-medium`, `live-footer-medium`) and A5's redrawn `live-*` frames: the tabs are equal in width to each other, at most 160px each, and centered as a group. The Plays room as it opens (`plays-mobile` / `-medium` / `-desktop` and the frames derived from them, A3's `plays-hidden-mobile` / `plays-scoring-hidden-mobile` and A4's `-nonotice` variants; DESIGN.md's `plays-mobile` row, its Interpretation "In the default view the game's last at-bat is the open row", and its Plays entry): the most recent inning with plays is still chosen, but every at-bat is collapsed; none is expanded until the viewer opens one.
**Repeals:** the last at-bat expanded by default.

A6 is unchanged: a video's play link still opens the Plays room on that play, with its at-bat expanded and marked by the bar.

## Frames

None. The v3.2.1 build shows it.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them, on 2026-11-12, in the site-polish list (issue #38): "tab menu on games should have a max width per tab and the tabs should be centered, and equal width to each other", and "the plays view shouldn't expand any plays by default". Shown the built pages as screenshots, on 2026-11-15: "Polish looks good. Yes amend them and ratify."

## Interpretation

- *Agent:* 160px is the maximum tab width chosen in the build; narrower screens shrink every tab equally, so they still fit in one row.
- *Agent:* "by default" means the room as it opens on its own. Opening from a video's play link is the viewer asking for that play, so A6 stands.
