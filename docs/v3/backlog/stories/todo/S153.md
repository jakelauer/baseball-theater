**Gap:** game-shell A6 (ratified 2026-11-19, owner: *"the one in figma looks good"*; asked whether the pill reaches Search: *"we already decided this and made it everywhere"*) draws Search's result rows with the inning pill (`search-playmeta-{mobile,medium,desktop}`): a result with a play reads its date, muted, then the pill (`Top 6 →`) linking to the play; a result with no play keeps Search's ratified `Highlight · <date>` in the primary text colour. S5 builds Search's rows as `kind · date` and its route returns no play id or inning, so nothing builds A6's Search rows (S5's notes, 2026-11-19).

**Impact:** Before S153, a Search result for a play could take you to its game but not to the play. S153 impact: a result for a play shows its inning as a small pill after the date (`Top 6 →`), and choosing it opens that play in the game's Plays room.

**Source:** Feature: Video play links
