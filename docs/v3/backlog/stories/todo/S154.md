**Gap:** The owner asked for the footer to carry a privacy policy, a user agreement and an about page (owner, 2026-11-10: *"The footer needs to have like a copyright and the year and probably a privacy policy and uh, there's another policy we always have. Uh, Like the user agreement or something."*; *"And about uh, and the contact."*), and, asked whether each should be its own story or left out, said to build them together (owner, 2026-11-19: *"add one story for all of them together, not one each"*). BT has none of these pages: `web/src/routes.tsx` serves no `/about`, `/privacy` or `/terms`, and S141's site footer (shell-scoreboard A14, a draft) draws their links, which S142 can't ship until a route serves them.

**Impact:** Before S154, a fan could not read what Baseball Theater is, what it does with their data, or the terms of using it. S154 impact: the footer's About, Privacy policy and Terms of use links open three pages whose words the owner approved.
