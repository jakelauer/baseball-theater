**Gap:** Backlog items are built one at a time, and most of what forces that is not real dependency between them (the 2026-11-19 proposal, `parallel-backlog-proposal.md`, measured on `stage` at `6aa1de14`). Every story writes the same few files: the ledger (`docs/v3/AUDIT.md`, appended by `scripts/audit.sh` at lines 171, 335, 396, 438 and 492; 285 commits in 30 days), `CHANGELOG.md` (new entries at the top; 98), the Current baseline's **As of** line (162) and three generated files that change whenever those do (`docs/v3/BACKLOG.md`, 336; `docs/v3/INDEX.md`, 148; `docs/v3/audit.jsonl`, 152), so any two branches cut from one base conflict, and `.claude/skills/run-backlog/SKILL.md` step 3 needs a paragraph of hand-merge rules. Every story adds `scripts/verify-S<N>.sh`, and all of `scripts/` is on `is_reviewer_path` (`scripts/claude-review/plan-review.py:52`), though no workflow runs a grader (`grep -rn verify- .github/` is empty): so every branch stacked above a story is held until it merges, and every merge from `stage` costs a paid full review (`plan-review.py:196–202`). Rule-file churn (`RULE_FILES`, `plan-review.py:48`; CLAUDE.md and LOOP.md, 100 commits in 30 days) forces a full re-review of every open pull request (`plan-review.py:191`). Workers share one working tree (`.claude/agents/story-worker.md`, `bug-worker.md`: "One at a time, never in parallel"), and ESLint in the main checkout lints other worktrees (#110: 24 parse errors; `eslint.config.js:17` ignores no `.claude/`; `.claude/worktrees/` is ignored only by the local `.git/info/exclude`, not the committed `.gitignore`). `story_diff` (`scripts/lib/checks.sh:78`) is a two-tree `git diff BASE TIP`, so a branch that merges `stage` in before its final `audit.sh` run counts other stories' files as its own. Review debt is counted as if one story ran at a time (`scripts/review-debt.sh`, 6 `done`). The 2026-11-19 run's eleven pull requests took about 28.5 hours to drain, about 23 minutes each once in order. The owner wants agents working side by side under a lead (owner, 2026-11-20: *"The goal is to enable agents to work in tandem on backlog items in a way that they don't conflict, and in a way that is managed by an overseer which can handle issues raised by the agents that may be interrelated or interdependent or require shared solutions. This is what an engineering lead would do for engineers on a team."*; then *"I want you to implement the parallelization changes"*).

**Impact:** Before S156, backlog work happened one item at a time, and a finished run spent about a day merging its pull requests one after another, because every item wrote the same few record files and every story counted as a change to the reviewer. S156 impact: each item's records are its own files, the generated backlog file is gone, a grader no longer holds the items above it, and a lead agent runs two (later three) items at once in separate copies of the repository, settling shared problems once and asking the owner one combined set of questions.
