**Gap:** `VideoCard` (`web/src/components/videos/VideoCard.tsx`) draws a clip's play link (`Play · Top 6th →`, S97) as its own line under the meta line, outside the card's button, and the meta line's first word comes from `KIND_LABELS` (`Highlight` for a highlight). The owner wants the link on the meta line instead (owner, 2026-11-10: *"Move hte play link to the 2nd line. Replace "Highlight" with "Play" in red. If there's no play, just keep "Highlight""*), and, shown S144's draft, as a pill after the date (owner, 2026-11-19: *"S144: move the link after the date, put the link in a pill inline, and change it to "inspect play""*; asked, *"Drop "Highlight ·""* and *"Player and Search"*; then *"The pill background should be subtly different from the background. Actually, instead of inspect play, use Top/Bot # The pill text should be the same color as the date"*), drawn by S144. The floating player (`PlayerDialog.tsx`) shows the same link as `Play · Top 6th →` in its overlay row.

**Impact:** Before S145, a video of a play had an extra line under it linking to the play. S145 impact: the card's second line gives the date and then a small pill with the inning ("Top 6 →") that takes you to the play, the video player shows the same pill, and a clip with no play still says "Highlight".
