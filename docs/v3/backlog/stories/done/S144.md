**Gap:** A video card whose clip has a play shows the play link on a line of its own under the meta line (`Play · Top 6th →`, game-shell [A5](./visual/game-shell/amendments/A5.md), ratified by S96 and built by S97 in `web/src/components/videos/VideoCard.tsx`), and the meta line reads `Highlight · Sep 5, 2026`. Looking at section V in Figma on 2026-11-10 the owner asked for a change (owner, 2026-11-10: *"Move hte play link to the 2nd line. Replace "Highlight" with "Play" in red. If there's no play, just keep "Highlight""*). Shown the draft on 2026-11-19 the owner changed it (owner, 2026-11-19: *"S144: move the link after the date, put the link in a pill inline, and change it to "inspect play""*), then, asked, dropped the kind word before the date on a play's line (*"Drop "Highlight ·""*), took the pill to the floating player and Search (*"Player and Search"*) and, in Search's floating player, put it after the game link in place of S127's At-Bat link (*"Game link + pill"*); shown the pill, they made it the inning in the date's colour on a background a shade off the card's (*"The pill background should be subtly different from the background. Actually, instead of inspect play, use Top/Bot # The pill text should be the same color as the date"*). A5 is ratified and built, so the change is a new amendment (ratified files are never edited, CLAUDE.md), and S145 builds it for the Videos room and the shared player.

**Impact:** Before S144, a video of a play had its link to the play on an extra line under the card. S144 impact: the owner approves a card whose second line gives the date and then a small pill with the inning ("Top 6 →") that takes you to the play, the same pill in the video player and in Search, and a clip with no play still says "Highlight".
